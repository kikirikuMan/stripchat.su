What are webcam couples

Webcam couples are broadcasts where there are two people in the frame at once. It’s interesting to watch how partners communicate with each other: they look at each other, joke, and react to each other. On Stripchat, couples' shows are collected in a separate feed, and its viewers are quite constant.

Many viewers say that they appreciate the naturalness here. Two people in the frame respond to the chat and are busy with each other, so the broadcast looks more like a piece of someone else’s evening than a production. Everything happens with the consent of both: the couple chooses the format, rules and boundaries of the broadcast themselves.

Finding couples is easy. They have their own feed in the Stripchat directory, and the broadcast section on this page already displays it. You can narrow the selection with the same filters used in other categories: region, language, age group and broadcast features.

How to choose a pair you'll like

From the preview in the feed you won’t understand much about the couple. It’s better to go on air for five to ten minutes and take a closer look at a few things.

  1. How partners communicate with each other. If the two of them are easy and fun together, it can be seen right away, and the show only benefits from it.
  2. Who responds in the chat and how. When questions are answered, the audience is clearly welcome here.
  3. Room goals and profile description. The profile usually lists the rules and what the couple will not do under any circumstances.
  4. Picture and sound. Two people need more space in the frame, so the difference between a good and a bad camera is more noticeable here than in a solo broadcast.

When couples are on air

Couples live in different time zones: among them there are many Europeans, Latin Americans, and there are also Russian-speaking duets. They usually air in the evening and at night local time, and most often on weekends when both have a free day. If a beloved couple disappears for a week, most likely one of the two has work or a trip.

A private show with a couple

A private show with a couple works the same way as with one model: you pay in tokens for time, and you get all the attention of both partners. The differences are in the little things. The couple agrees in advance that they are ready to show, and the rules of privacy in her profile are usually described in detail. Read them before you start so as not to ask for too much.

And one more thing: in a private show, one partner often conducts the conversation while the other is busy with the show. If the answer is not the one to whom you addressed, there is no need to be offended, this is how their roles are distributed.

How to behave in a couple's chat

In a couple's room, etiquette is a little more complicated than in a solo broadcast. Contact both. If you notice only one, the other is unpleasant, and the mood in the room quickly deteriorates. Don't try to "prank" the couple and don't ask about things they might quarrel about, such as exes. It is also better to address compliments to two people: “you look great together” sounds more pleasant than praise to one person.

If you want to support the broadcast, use tips and room goals, which are described in detail in the guide about tips. The couple often skips requests in the general chat without a tip; she has her own program for the evening.
