What styles are most common

Music and track order

In dance live broadcasts, the nice thing is that the playlist can be influenced. Many models in the menu tips There is the item "Dance to your song" or "Choose a track". It usually looks like this:

  1. You open the tip menu and find the item about music or dance.
  2. Send the specified tips and write the track name to the chat.
  3. The model puts on a song and dances. If she does not have such a track, she will offer a similar one or ask to choose another one.

The model sets the price of such items herself, so see her menu. If you haven't figured out the domestic currency yet, take a look at the guide on tokens.

Dance live broadcast etiquette

  • Do not interrupt the number with questions: the model will see them when they finish and answer.

  • Offer tracks politely and remember that the final choice is up to the model.
