How to communicate with a sports model

Fitness models spend a lot of time on the form, and they are pleased when viewers notice it. A couple of tips:

  • Praise the specific. A compliment to technique or stretching the model will appreciate more than general words.
  • Ask about sports. It's easy to start a conversation with the question of your favorite exercise or program.
  • Do not criticize the figure. Tips like “you should pump up this or that” are inappropriate, even if you yourself understand fitness.
  • Don't distract during approach. Wait until the model finishes the exercise and then ask.
