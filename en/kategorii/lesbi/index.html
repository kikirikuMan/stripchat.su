Lesbian show: two characters in one broadcast

Lesbian shows are webcam broadcasts in which there are two adult girls in the frame at once. These could be friends, a couple or colleagues who decided to broadcast together. What distinguishes good duets is that the girls are at ease with each other, and the audience immediately sees this.

Compared to solo broadcasts, there is more movement here. When there are two people in the frame, the broadcast does not sag: the girls talk, make fun of each other, read the chat together and come up with something to keep the audience occupied. It’s interesting to watch such a duet, even if you don’t write anything yourself.

What are duets

  • Regular couples

    Girls who are together in life. Their broadcasts contain a lot of tenderness, common jokes and stories about joint trips.

  • Girlfriends

    Easier format: a lot of laughter and chat, and the chat often gives ideas.

  • Contrasting duets

    Blonde and brunette, calm and lively. Viewers like that they can choose “yours” and watch both at the same time.

  • Guest broadcasts

    A model who usually works solo invites her friend for one evening. Such broadcasts are worth catching: they happen infrequently.

How to find lesbian shows

The selection at the top of the page already shows broadcasts on the topic. You can search it yourself like this:

  1. In the Stripchat directory, open the couples section or select a tag indicating a two-girl show.
  2. Look at the preview: you can immediately see how many people are in the frame.
  3. Read the profile description: duets often write their names and schedule of joint broadcasts there.
  4. Add your favorite duet to your favorites to receive notifications when the girls go live.

If you are interested in broadcasts with several participants in general, also take a look at the pairs category: different formats of joint broadcasts are collected there.

Lesbian show formats

Games with chat

"Truth or Dare", quizzes, tasks from the audience. It’s more interesting to play together: girls compete, argue and root for each other.

Joint goals

The girls announce the goal, the chat collects tips, and when the amount is reached, the duo fulfills their promise. Even small tips here go into the general pot, and you participate in the show along with everyone else.

Dancing and fittings

Synchronized dancing, display of images, exchange of outfits. These are usually the moments where people laugh the most on air.

Private with a duet

In a private show, both girls pay attention only to you. The cost and rules are indicated in the profile, and you can read more about how this format works in the guide about private shows.

How to choose a duet according to your mood

Lesbian shows are very different, and whether the broadcast is right for you is clear in the first minutes. For a relaxing evening, look for couples who talk more, they usually have quiet music and a leisurely pace. If you are in a cheerful mood, choose friends with games and goals. There's always something going on there, and the chat is readily available.

Pay attention to the language. Duets from different countries often communicate with each other in the same language, and with chat in English. If speaking without an interpreter is important to you, mark the desired language in the filters. And lovers of contrast should look for couples where the girls are noticeably different in appearance: for example, a redhead and a brunette, or an athletic one and a petite one.

A good broadcast can be seen by the way the girls treat the audience. If they greet new guests, answer questions, and thank you for their support, you'll likely enjoy your stay.

Etiquette on air duet

They communicate with two models at once a little differently than on a solo broadcast:

  • Respect both. Do not write openly that you like one better, this can easily ruin the mood of the entire broadcast.
  • If the question is addressed to one of the girls, call her by name, this will make it easier for them to understand who to answer.
  • If the girls are a couple, their relationship concerns only them. It’s better not to ask about life off the air.
  • Don't demand it. The girls decide for themselves what to do on public air, and special wishes are discussed through goals or privately.

General rules of conduct in chat are collected in the webcam etiquette guide, it is useful to flip through it before your first visit.

Why viewers love this format

There are two interlocutors in the lesbian show, and therefore the conversation does not subside. The girls support each other, behave more freely, and the broadcast resembles a friendly party where you were invited too. Many people come here out of curiosity, and then return to their favorite duets.

All participants in the broadcasts are adults and go on air voluntarily. Public shows are open for free and without registration, so choose a duet that suits your mood and see what the girls have come up with for today.
