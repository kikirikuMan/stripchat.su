Tattoo Models

Girls with tattoos in webcam chat stand out from the first frame. One has a sleeve made of flowers, the other has a thin inscription on the collarbone, the third graph is full on the back. There is a story behind almost every drawing, and models willingly tell them in a live broadcast.

This category is chosen by those who are close to an alternative culture like rock, punk or gothic, or who just likes a bold attitude to their own appearance. Tattooed models are rarely “like everyone else”, this is heard from the music on the background and can be seen in how they communicate and decorate the room.

What styles meet

There are many directions in the tattoo, and almost any in live broadcasts. Most often, viewers look for such images:

    Colored hair, piercing, tattoos on the hands and neck, heavy boots. Rock often plays in the background.

  • minimalism

    a few neat subtle drawings that you do not notice right away. For those who love details.

  • scale work

    sleeves, back, thigh in color or blackwork. Such models often tell how many sessions it took.

  • tattoo and cosplay

    some models weave their drawings into the character image. If you're interested, check the cosplay.

it is curious to follow and how the collection is replenished. Many models regularly make new works and share this with the audience: they show a sketch, tell how the session went, talk about the next picture. Permanent viewers over time know every tattoo and its story. There is something like a series with a sequel that you can watch live, and sometimes suggest the following plot.

What to talk about with a tattooed model

it's convenient to start a conversation with tattoos, and the models know it. The question "What does this drawing mean?" It almost always leads to a story about a trip, about a person, or about some important point. The following topics go well in the chat:

  • the first tattoo and as a model decided on it;
  • what drawing was the hardest;
  • plans for new works and sketch ideas;
  • Music, films and subcultures that inspire her style.

If you also have tattoos, tell us about them. You get closer to common interests, and the model will begin to recognize you among the audience.

What you should not write to chat

even benevolent interest Sometimes it sounds tactless. A couple of simple rules will help to avoid this:

  • do not evaluate tattoos in the spirit of "why spoiled yourself." The model herself decided how to look, and criticism is inappropriate here.
  • Don't demand to show a picture that is where the model is not ready to show in a public live broadcast. For special requests, there is a private show.
  • Don't write the same thing many times in a row: the model sees the chat and answers when it can.
  • If you like the live broadcast, support the model ticup. So the easiest way to show that you value her time.
