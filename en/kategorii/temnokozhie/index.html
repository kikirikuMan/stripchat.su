Why picture quality is more important here

Webcams and video compression algorithms historically transmit darker tones worse. In low light, the face loses details, and with unsuccessful settings, the skin looks grayish. Experienced models know this and neatly set the light, but the difference between a good and average live stream in this category is more noticeable than in others.

So if the picture is important to you, choose broadcasts in high resolution and with good lighting. Switch the video quality manually on the slow internet: the image will become more stable, although a little less detailed.

Respect in the chat

The compliment of appearance is appropriate if it is addressed to a person, without the intonation “Look what exotic”. Ask where the model leads the live broadcast, what kind of music she listens to, what she advises to watch in her city. Offensive words and “jokes” about skin color end at once, and rightly so.
