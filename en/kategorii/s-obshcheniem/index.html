Symptoms of a talkative model

At the preview about the character of a person, almost nothing is understood, but in a few minutes a lot of things become clear in the room:

  • The model itself asks the chat questions;
  • remembers regular viewers and addresses them by nicknames;
  • the microphone is on, the sound is clear, the music does not drown out the voice;
  • the profile description mentions hobbies, books or series, that is, there is something about talk;
  • there are few spectators in the room: in a small live broadcast, it's easier to wait for an answer.

How to start a conversation and not get lost

beginners often go into the room and immediately write "hello, how are you?". The model hears this dozens of times a evening. It is better to cling to something specific: the music that plays, the thing on the shelf, the theme that has just been discussed. Such a message will be noticed, and the models will be seen that you are really watching a live broadcast.

Do not rush. Dating in webcam chat is progressive. At first you are one of the many, then the model begins to recognize you, and after several visits you already have common themes. Small tips will help to speed things up with a question, it's customary on the site.

voice, pause and conversation pace

In webcam communication, the model usually responds with voice, and you write in text, so the conversation is with a slight delay. While she is reading your message, you are already typing the following. If there is no answer yet, wait with new questions and let the models finish. Sounds like a correspondence with a friend who answers aloud and pauses are common here.
