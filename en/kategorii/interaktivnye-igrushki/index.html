What are interactive toys in webcam

Interactive toys are devices connected to the model’s broadcast and respond to viewers’ tips. You send tokens, the device immediately fires, the model reacts, and in the chat you can see that you did it.

The category is popular precisely because of this instant impact. You watch a regular broadcast from the outside, but here the viewer himself influences what happens in the frame. After this format, regular broadcasts seem too calm to many.

How it works

The viewer will not have to understand the devices, the diagram is simple:

  1. The model connects her device to the broadcast through a special application or built-in platform integration.
  2. It customizes the menu: what tip size includes what level, how many seconds the trigger lasts, are there any special modes.
  3. The viewer sends a tip from his token balance.
  4. The device operates according to the menu, and a notification appears in the chat with the sender's nickname.

When tips are sent by several viewers in a row, the tips usually fall into a queue. So your tokens sometimes “arrive” in a few seconds, and this is not considered a failure.

How to read the tip menu

Each model has its own menu, but it is structured similarly. Typically this is a list of levels from weak to strong, each with a price in tokens and duration. Sometimes there are special items on the menu:

  • Wave or pattern

    The trigger force changes as you go. Such modes are often marked with a separate name.

  • Random level

    The amount is fixed, but the intensity is random. Liked by those who love surprises.

  • Direct control

    The viewer temporarily gains control of the device. More often available privately or for a fee.

  • Common goal

    The chat together collects an amount, after which the model turns on a special mode or performs an announced action.

Prices and levels are set by the model itself, so always look at its menu before sending a tip. It is usually located next to the broadcast window or in the profile description.

Interactive toys in public and private shows

In a public broadcast, the device responds to tips from all viewers at once. It turns out to be a general game: someone turns on a weak level, someone turns on a strong one, triggers come one after another, and the model reacts to each one. The room is noisy and exciting, and the show consists of the actions of different people.

In a private show there is no queue, the model is occupied only by you, and some models in private shows give the viewer direct control of the device for the duration of the session. This format is chosen by those who value personal contact and complete control over the pace. Each model has its own conditions, so read her profile before starting, and the general rules for private shows are collected in the guide about private shows.

The model can always stop the device, pause or turn off the interaction. This is her right, and there is no point in arguing with this in chat.

Etiquette in interactive broadcasts

  • Don't ask for a "free to include" model. Its rules are written in the menu.
  • Do not bombard the chat with questions about why the trigger was delayed. Chances are your tips are in line.
  • If the model calls a pause or break, wait. The device does not have to work the entire broadcast.
  • In addition to tokens, write a few words to the model in response to the reaction. She also enjoys communication.

Frequently asked questions

How does an interactive toy work on air?

The model's device is connected to the broadcast. When a viewer sends a tip, it is triggered with the strength and duration that the model has preset in its menu.

How much does it cost to activate a toy?

Each model decides this. Levels and their cost in tokens are indicated in the tip menu on the broadcast page.

Can the device be controlled directly?

Some models offer direct control, usually privately or for a separate tip. The conditions are described in their profile or menu.

Can other viewers see who activated the toy?

Usually a notification appears in the chat with a nickname and level. If you want to remain invisible, use a neutral nickname.
