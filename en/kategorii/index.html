How categories work on Stripchat

There are thousands of models online at the same time, and you can spend hours scrolling through the general feed at random. Categories and filters allow you to leave in a minute only those broadcasts that are interesting to you personally.

The first and broadest division on Stripchat is by broadcast type: women, couples, men and trans models. These are four separate feeds, each with its own audience and show formats. Within each feed, you can narrow the results by appearance, age group, region and language, body type, and features such as HD, VR or interactive toys.

Please note that only those who are on air right now are visible in the category. The list changes all the time. In the morning in Moscow there are more models from Asia, in the evening from Russia and Europe, and late at night from Latin America. If your favorite category is empty now, check back in a couple of hours.

How to choose your category

  • By the language of communication

    If you want to talk more, start with the language. The easiest way to look for Russian-speaking models is in the Russian girls section.

  • By broadcast format

    Couples, solo shows, dancing, cosplay, broadcasts with interactive toys. The tempo and dynamics of these formats are noticeably different.

  • By appearance and age

    Hair color, origin, body type, age group. All models on the site are adults, and age filters only help you find a type close to you.

  • According to technical conditions

    For those who watch from a phone or with a weak Internet, selections based on video quality and mobile broadcasts will be useful.

Filters can be safely combined. Requests like “Russian-language model with an interactive toy” or “couple in HD” are quite common, and filters were invented for them. Found a model you like? Add it to your favorites. To do this, you need a free account; how to create one is described in registration guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to select several filters at once?

You can. On Stripchat, filters are combined: say, set the language, age group and broadcast type at the same time, and only models that match all conditions will remain in the list.

Why is the list of models changing in the category?

Only those who are currently on air are visible in the category. Models come and go throughout the day, so the composition of the list depends on the time of day.
