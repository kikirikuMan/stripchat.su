Popular webcam models: who is in the top and why

Home›Categories›Popular models

Where are the most viewers now

Top webcams are models that currently attract the most viewers. Their broadcasts are at the beginning of the feed, the chat in such rooms does not stop, and the goals are closed one after another. If you are watching a webcam for the first time, it is convenient to start with the top: it is immediately clear what a popular broadcast looks like, where everything is in order with light and sound and the chat is live.

The top also has disadvantages. Below is how popularity develops on webcam sites, how a large room differs from a small one, and how to find models who deserve the top, but have not yet reached it.

Where does popularity come from

The sites rarely tell you exactly how ratings are calculated, but you can understand the general logic. The place of a model in the feed and in the top lists is usually influenced by the following:

  • Spectators in the room

    The most obvious indicator. Sorting “by popularity” most often takes into account how many people are watching the broadcast at the moment.

  • Chat activity

    Messages, tips, and closed goals show that viewers are actually following the broadcast, rather than keeping a tab open in the background.

  • Regularity

    Models that go on air on a schedule have regular viewers: they know when to come.

  • On-air time

    The longer and more stable the broadcasts are, the higher the chance that the model will be noticed by new viewers.

Many sites also organize ratings and competitions among models from time to time. Their conditions change; it is better to look at the current rules on the site itself. How tips help your favorite model rise higher is written in the about tips guide.

How to watch the best webcams and not get lost

  1. Open the general feed and select sorting by popularity, if available in the catalog.
  2. You should not enter all rooms in a row. Choose two or three and spend a few minutes in each.
  3. Read the profile description and tip menu. For popular models, everything is written out there, so you will save time.
  4. In a busy chat, write briefly, a long message will go up in a couple of seconds.
  5. Add the broadcasts you like to your favorites, otherwise you will have to look for them in the constantly changing top.

Why the top webcam models change all the time

The popularity rating is updated in real time and looks more like a board at a train station than a list of winners. The model who had a full room in the evening may be offline in the morning, and her place will be taken by the one whose broadcast has just started. Time zones also have an effect. When it’s night in Europe, models from Latin America rise to the top, and in the evening in Moscow there are more Russian-language broadcasts in the top.

So take the top calmly. If your favorite model is not in the forefront today, most likely she just went on air at an unusual time, and her show didn’t get worse because of it.

Underrated models: where to look

Any model from the top once started with an empty room, and the most interesting finds are often found just below the top of the feed. Here's where to look for them:

  • For beginners. Fresh faces are collected in the new models section. Beginners are especially happy to see every viewer.
  • Next to the first screen. Models in the middle of the feed are often in no way inferior to the leaders, they just now have fewer viewers.
  • Through filters. If you set the language, region or type of show, you will find streams that get lost in the general feed.
  • During off-peak hours. During the day and late at night, there is less competition for attention, and other models get to the top.

Frequently asked questions

How does a model get to the top?

Several things influence this: how many people are in the room, how active the chat is, how regularly the model goes on air and how fans support her. The exact formulas for site ratings are usually kept to themselves.

If a model is in the top, is she the best?

Not necessarily. The top shows only who is being watched the most at that moment. You may be more suited to a model in a small room where she has time for each viewer.

Why is the composition of the top constantly changing?

The broadcasts take place in real time: models go on air and end it, viewers move from room to room. Because of this, the top of the tape looks completely different in the morning and evening.

Is it possible to watch top models for free?

Public broadcasts are open to everyone without payment. Tokens will be needed if you want to leave a tip, contribute to the room, or invite a model to a private show.
