Separate tape for trans models

Transmodels on Stripchat perform in their own feed, along with girls, couples and guys. The category has its own stars, regular viewers and long-established communication habits.

A separate feed is convenient for both sides. Viewers looking for trans models can find them directly, and models do not have to explain who they are in every broadcast. Adult trans women, trans men and non-binary people broadcast here, although trans women traditionally make up most of the feed.

Many trance models gather the audience for years and know regular viewers by nicknames. Such rooms are like a club with their regulars. A newcomer is accepted there willingly, if the first reports show that he came with good intentions.

Respect in the chat

Politeness in this category is especially appreciated. Trans models often encounter rudeness on the Internet, so viewers who behave like a human being are remembered, and others are quickly expelled. A few rules should be kept in mind:

  • Contact as indicated in the profile. The model itself chooses the name and gender in which it is spoken of. She says "she" means "she".
  • Don't ask medical questions. Hormones, operations, “what you used to be like” refer to personal, and in the general chat, such questions sound tactless.
  • Don't use offensive words, even if it seems to be a joke or "everyone says so." Neutral "trans-model" and "trans-girl" are understandable to everyone.
  • Compliment the same as any other model: image, smile, voice, sense of humor.

General rules of conduct in the chat are collected in a guide to webcam etiquette. In this category, they should be observed especially carefully.
