What is VR broadcasts

VR broadcasts are webcam live broadcasts that shoot with a wide viewing stereo camera. In the virtual reality helmet, the picture becomes voluminous, and there is a feeling that you are sitting in the same room with the model.

For this sense of presence, the format is usually tried. You can turn your head and look around, estimate the distance, see the interior around. In the usual broadcast, you see a flat frame, in a VR live broadcast you can see the whole room.

VR equipment models are fewer than most other categories because stereo camera and good light cost money. Those who still shoot in VR, the quality of the live broadcast is usually taken seriously.

what you need to view

  • autonomous helmet

    The easiest option: a headset with a built-in browser. Open the site, start the broadcast, turn on the VR mode.

  • PC helmet

    Helmet connected to a computer. The broadcast is launched in a browser on a VR-enabled PC and is output to the headset.

  • Smartphone and simple glasses

    Budget: The phone is inserted into the lens holder. The dive is weaker, but the volume is still felt.

  • without helmet

    VR live streaming also opens in a normal browser as a panorama that can be rotated. It's so convenient to check if you like the format.

A separate application is usually not needed, everything works in the browser. Just make sure the helmet browser is updated.

How to start a VR live in a helmet

  1. Put on a helmet and open the built-in browser.
  2. Go to the site and find VR broadcasts: there is a separate category or tag in the stripchat directory.
  3. Select a model and open its live stream.
  4. Press the VR mode button in the player or the glasses icon. If the browser asks for permission to dive, confirm.
  5. Does the picture double or does it look flat? Check the display format in the player settings.

To write directly from the helmet, it is more convenient to log in to your account on your computer or phone first, and then log in to the headset browser. If there is no account yet, it will come in handy Registration Guide.

Internet and picture quality

VR video is noticeably heavier than usual: the frame is wider, and the image is transmitted at once for two eyes. Therefore, almost everything depends on the connection.

  • If the router supports the 5 GHz band, connect the helmet to it. There is less interference, the speed is higher.
  • Sit closer to the router. The walls and distance cut the speed, and the braking in the helmet is more annoying than on the screen.
  • At the time of viewing, pause download, update, and video on other devices.
  • If a live stream stutters, lower the resolution in the player. In VR, smoothness is more important than clarity.

If the broadcast does not start at all, take a look at the section on what to do if it does not load. And if the maximum clarity in the usual format is important to you, there is a category HD broadcasts.

What is the difference between VR live broadcasting from normal

First of all, the feeling of distance. A stereo camera stands where your eyes would be if you were sitting in this room. When the model looks into the lens or leans closer, the helmet seems to be addressing you personally. Experienced VR models take this into account: they keep a comfortable distance, do not make sudden movements near the camera and think about what will be around the edges of the view.

Communication changes too. Printing in a helmet is not as convenient as on a keyboard, so viewers write shorter, and models are more often asked questions that can be answered in one word. Some people use voice input in the headset browser. If the conversation is important to you, keep your phone with an open chat next to it and write from it without taking off your helmet.

Tips for comfortable viewing

If you have a helmet recently, start with short sessions: out of habit, a long time can make your head spin. Sit so that you don't have to turn all the time, and keep the lenses clean.

The rules in the VR live chat are the same as everywhere else. Be polite, do not put pressure on the model, but leave special wishes for tips or a private show. VR models often ask how the viewer looks like a picture, so your quality review will really come in handy for them.

VR broadcasts are still a little, but they make a strong impression. If you have a helmet, try at least one live broadcast, it's hard to describe the feeling of presence in words.

Frequently asked questions

what helmet is needed for VR broadcasts?

A headset with a built-in browser is suitable, which supports VR mode, such as a stand-alone helmet. PC helmets also work through a browser on a computer.

Is it possible to watch VR live broadcasts without a helmet?

possible. In a normal browser, such a broadcast opens as a panoramic video that is rotated with a mouse or finger, only there will be no volume.

Do I need to install the application?

usually not. It is enough to open the site in the helmet browser and turn on the VR mode in the broadcast player.

Why does VR live broadcast slow?

voluminous video needs more traffic than normal. Connect the helmet to fast Wi-Fi, preferably in the 5 GHz band, and close other downloads.
