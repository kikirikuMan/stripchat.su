how to save mobile traffic

video Eats the most traffic, and a long browsing through the mobile Internet can significantly reduce the package. Several habits help spend less:

  • Choose the quality manually. On a small screen, the difference between high and medium resolution is almost invisible, and the traffic goes many times less.
  • Connect to Wi-Fi, and leave the mobile Internet for short visits to road.
  • Close the broadcast completely. In some browsers, the collapsed video continues to load in the background tab.
