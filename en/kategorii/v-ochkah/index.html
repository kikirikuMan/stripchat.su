Glasses as part of the image

Webcam in glasses is a small niche, but you can recognize it at first sight. The frame changes the face more than makeup: the same model in glasses can look like a strict teacher, and in another frame, the heroine of an old film. For this change of images of girls in glasses and love.

The glasses just seem charming to one viewer. Others like the contrast of the serious frame and playful nature of the model. Whatever the reason, in the ribbon above it is easy to find a frame to taste.

Why do glasses look so good on the camera

The camera brings the face closer, and any detail on it becomes more noticeable than in life. Glasses attract attention to the eyes, and the look into the camera for the webcam model is the most important way to communicate. When she looks over the frame directly into the lens, she seems to be addressing you personally.

There is also a domestic reason. Many models read chat from the screen and simply do not see small text without glasses. So if a girl puts on glasses, then she takes it off, perhaps she is just reading your message.

What little things a lover of glasses can see

If the glasses for the model are part of the style, it is noticeable in detail. There are several frames next to the camera, and from a live broadcast to a live broadcast, it changes them to the mood. Makeup is chosen so that the eyes are not lost behind the glass. The light is exposed without sharp glare, and this is not easy to achieve, so experienced models are proud of this skill.

It's different: the model puts glasses only on part of the live broadcast, for example, when reading questions from the audience or having a themed evening. If you need an image in glasses, look at such models more often, and sooner or later you will get to the right moment.

A couple of chat ideas

It is convenient to start a conversation with glasses without slipping into banality. Ask how many frames the model has and what is her favorite, how long has she worn glasses. You can offer to arrange a "fitting" of several pairs right in the live broadcast. Many models willingly pick up such a topic, because attention here is unobtrusive.

  • Praise the style and do not ask to take off or put on glasses every five minutes.
  • If you want a specific look, look at the tip menu: there are items like changing the outfit.
  • Don't joke about vision and "nerds". It may seem harmless to you, and the model, most likely, has already heard many times.
