Male side of webcam

Webcam guys is a separate Stripchat feed where men perform in front of the camera. The category is smaller than the girl category, but it has a regular audience and a noticeable bias towards conversational, sports and even gaming broadcasts.

Male broadcasts are watched by very different people. Women who want to chat without pressure and obligations. Men who like men. Couples who choose to broadcast together. Models know this and try to make everyone in the room comfortable.

If you are opening a men's feed for the first time, you can just look at it first. No one will ask why you came in, and public broadcasts are available even without an account. Select the broadcast in the block above and decide for yourself whether you like it or not.

Who performs in this category

There are many types, and with filters you can quickly find yours. More often than others you come across these:

    Guys who keep in shape and willingly show workouts or talk about nutrition. Fans of sports aesthetics will also find the sports models section useful.

  • Interlocutors

    Models who like to talk more. They discuss films, trips and relationships, ask questions, joke.

  • Creative

    Musicians, dancers, lovers of cosplay and unusual images.

  • Mature men

    A niche for those who like older men, calm and confident. Of course, all models in this category are adults.

How to find a suitable broadcast

  1. Switch to the guys' feed at the top of the broadcast directory. The block with broadcasts on this page already shows it.
  2. Narrow down your selection using filters: age group, body type, region, language spoken.
  3. Look at the name of the room. Many guys write in it what the broadcast is dedicated to today: training, games, just chatting.
  4. Check your profile to find out the schedule and room rules.

It's easier here for beginners. The chats in the men's feed are usually calmer than in popular girls' rooms, the message is not lost in the flow, and the model often has time to respond personally.

Typical show formats

There are more formats in the men's feed than you might expect. One guy turns on the camera during the workout and answers questions between sets. Another cooks dinner and dictates the recipe along the way. There are broadcasts with interactive toys, where the model’s reaction depends on tips, and shows with room goals: when viewers collect the required amount of tokens, the model fulfills its promise.

Private shows work the same as in other categories: you pay in tokens for one-on-one time, and the model pays attention only to you. If private is new to you, first take a look at the guide to private shows, it will explain step by step how it works and what you should know in advance.

When guys are online

The men's band is smaller, so fluctuations during the day are more noticeable. Most of the broadcasts are in the evening according to European time, that is, closer to the night in Moscow. During the day and early morning the selection is smaller, but at this time models are usually in no hurry and are more willing to chat. Add the guy you like to your favorites, so it will be visible when he is online.

Safety and personal boundaries of the viewer

The site rules also work in favor of the spectators. Don't send your photos or turn on your camera unless you're sure you want to. Do not follow links that are sent to you in private messages, and do not agree to go to instant messengers. Honest models work only on the site, and scammers are often behind the offer to “chat in another place.”

You can add an intrusive interlocutor in a chat to ignore or complain about him to the room moderator. A smart model keeps order herself and prevents the audience from hurting each other.

Advice for spectators

  • The model doesn't know your gender or who you are. She only sees her nickname in the chat, and she doesn’t need to indicate anything else in her profile. Read more in the guide about anonymity.
  • Start with something simple: say hello and ask about what’s happening in the frame. This is the easiest question to start a conversation with.
  • Girls have no need to be shy. Many male models admit that they find it most pleasant to talk to female spectators.
  • Respect boundaries. “No” from the model means no, persuasion is useless.
