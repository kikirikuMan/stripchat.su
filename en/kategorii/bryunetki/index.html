The largest selection in the feed

There are more brunettes online than any other models on any webcam service, and Stripchat is no exception. Dark hair is found on models from Europe, Latin America, Asia, the Middle East and Russia, so the category is almost never empty.

This can make it difficult to choose. Open the “dark hair” filter without other qualifications, and you will get hundreds of previews that differ more from each other than blondes and redheads. Therefore, further discussion will mainly focus on how to narrow down the choice.

But whatever type you like, you will almost certainly find it among brunettes right now, and you won’t have to wait for the right time. All you have to do is set up the filters and take your time.

What types of brunettes are there

The hair color is the same, but everyone has their own images and manner of broadcasting:

  • Elegant style

    Strict outfits, neat styling, calm manner of communication. Such broadcasts are reminiscent of a conversation over a glass of wine.

  • Southern temperament

    Models from Spain, Italy, Colombia and Brazil: loud laughter, music, dancing. Read more about this energy on the page Latin American women.

  • Alternative look

    Black hair, piercings, tattoos, gothic or punk aesthetics. If this is your thing, take a look at the models with tattoos section.

  • The girl next door

    Natural appearance and minimal makeup, broadcast from an ordinary room. People come here for a simple conversation.

When to enter

The selection of brunettes is filled almost around the clock, only its geography changes. In the morning and afternoon in Moscow, the feed contains more models from Asia and Eastern Europe, in the evening they are joined by Russia and Western Europe, and at night and until the early morning, Latin America is most active. If you're looking for a specific type, time of day matters just as much as filters. It is easier to catch models from Southern Europe in the evening, and from Latin America in the dead of night.

Russian-speaking brunettes are most often on air from evening until late at night in Moscow, especially on weekends.

First minutes in the room

With a large selection, it’s easy to get stuck in endless scrolling: you went on air for ten seconds and left. It’s better to note three or four previews that caught your attention and sit in each room for at least a couple of minutes. Look:

  • how does the model react to chat, does it use nicknames and maintains a conversation;
  • is there a purpose in the room and what is written in the description;
  • do you like the music, light and tempo of the broadcast?

Hair color helps weed out unnecessary things, and people usually choose “their” model based on how she communicates.

In such a large category, favorites help out. If you find a model that you want to return to, save it immediately. Otherwise, you won’t be able to find it among hundreds of similar previews, especially if you don’t remember the nickname. Favorites require a free account.

How to start a conversation

In a popular room, dozens of viewers are writing at the same time, and messages like “hi, beautiful” are drowned without a trace. They notice specific questions: about music, a book on a shelf, or the country from which the model is broadcasting. Brunettes from different countries willingly talk about their city, and listening to it is much more interesting than routine compliments.
