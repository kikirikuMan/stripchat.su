How to choose a room

There are many broadcasts online at the same time, and the previews in the feed say little about the person. Filters and a few minutes of observation help.

  • Language of communication

    If you want Russian erotic chat, set the language or region in the filters: this way you won’t have to explain yourself through a translator.

  • Age and type

    Some prefer girls, others prefer mature, confident women. For the latter, there is a separate selection of mature models.

  • Broadcast format

    Solo broadcast of one model and a show together feel completely different. If you are interested in the latter, take a look at the pair category.

  • Order in the chat

    See if the model responds to the audience and if there is any rudeness in the room. If the chat has turned into a dump, it is unlikely that you will be able to have a normal conversation there.

It’s a good sign if the model greets new viewers and remembers regular ones. With this model, the conversation is interesting in itself, and not just for the sake of the counter.

How to behave so that you are welcome

The rules here are almost the same as in ordinary politeness. Say hello, address the model by nickname, and do not flood the chat with identical messages. Do not demand what the model has directly excluded in her profile, and do not bargain in the general feed: she sets the prices and conditions herself. If you ask for something, support the request with a tip or the purpose of the room. Models and moderators quickly suppress rudeness and intrusiveness, up to blocking the room.

In front of you is a living person at work. The model also gets tired, is not in the mood and goes on breaks. She often remembers a kind word in a chat better than a large tip.

Viewer privacy

The viewer's camera in erotic chat is turned off, the model and other participants see only your nickname. Come up with a nickname that is not associated with your real name; do not write your phone number, address, place of work, or links to social networks in the chat. If you are using a shared computer, log out after viewing or watch in incognito mode. More practical advice is collected in the guide about anonymity.
