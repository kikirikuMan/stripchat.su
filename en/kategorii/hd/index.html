What is HD broadcasting

HD broadcasts are webcam broadcasts in high definition, with a good camera, good lighting and a stable stream. In this quality, facial features, the texture of fabric and the shine of jewelry are visible, which in a normal broadcast blur into pixels.

Models that broadcast in HD usually take broadcasting seriously. To get a good picture, it’s not enough to buy an expensive camera; you also need to set the light, choose a background and set up a program for streaming. Therefore, this category often includes the most well-kept and beautifully decorated rooms on the site.

What makes up the quality of the broadcast

The HD icon only indicates the resolution. How your broadcast will look depends on several things:

  • Model camera

    The better the matrix and lens, the clearer the picture, especially in low light.

  • Light

    Even a great camera makes noise in the dark. Professional models use ring lamps and softboxes.

  • Internet model

    To transmit an HD stream, the model needs a fast and stable output channel.

  • Your connection

    If there is not enough speed on your side, the player itself will reduce the quality or begin to slow down.

How to find high-quality broadcasts

This page shows a selection of popular broadcasts. To watch only HD, use the Stripchat directory:

  1. Turn on the quality filter or select the HD tag in the catalog.
  2. Pay attention to the quality icon on the broadcast preview.
  3. Add other parameters to HD: appearance, language or show format. In high quality, dance broadcasts especially benefit, where every movement is important.
  4. Once you open the broadcast, check your player settings: sometimes the default quality is set to automatic.

How to set up Internet for HD

Most often the quality sags due to the connection of the viewer. To ensure smooth HD broadcasting, try the following:

  • If you are watching from a computer, connect with a cable. A wired connection is almost always more stable than Wi-Fi.
  • Switch to 5 GHz Wi-Fi. This range is faster and suffers less from neighboring networks, although it passes through walls worse.
  • Free up the channel. Speed is taken away by torrents, system updates and videos on other devices in the house.
  • Update your browser. The latest versions work better with modern video codecs and load the processor less.
  • Close unnecessary tabs. Several broadcasts immediately consume both traffic and computer resources.

If the picture does not load at all or there is a black screen, take a look at common loading problems.

Player settings

In most broadcasts, you can select the quality manually. In automatic mode, the player itself adapts to the connection speed, and this is convenient. True, with an unstable Internet, the automation “jumps” between resolutions, and the picture is either clear or blurry. Then it’s better to manually set the level slightly below the maximum, and the image will stop jumping.

Expand the broadcast to full screen. In a small window, the difference between HD and standard quality is difficult to notice, but on the entire monitor screen it catches your eye immediately.

Where the difference in quality is most noticeable

In some broadcasts, high resolution is almost imperceptible, in others, without it, much is lost. It gives the most in such cases:

  • Dancing and training. During fast movements, the normal picture is blurred, but in HD it remains clear.
  • Costumes and makeup. The details of the cosplay or evening dress that the model worked hard on are visible only in good quality.
  • Decoration of the room. Garlands and low-resolution colored light merge into spots.
  • Private show. When a model communicates only with you, you want to see her facial expressions and reaction to every message. More details about the format are described in the guide about private shows.

If the broadcast is in the background in a small window while you are busy with something else, maximum quality is useless. Standard resolution will be enough, and at the same time you will save traffic and computer resources.

HD on your phone

High quality broadcasts are also available from a smartphone, just watch the traffic. On mobile Internet, HD quickly eats up the packet, and for long viewing it is better to connect to Wi-Fi. More details about the features of mobile viewing are described on the page broadcasts from a phone.

Is it worth watching in HD

In high quality, the broadcast is more like a live conversation: you can see facial expressions, a smile and how the model reacts to your message. The light and decoration of the room are also lost in a grainy picture. If your connection allows, watch at least one broadcast in HD on a big screen and compare with regular quality for yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How to distinguish an HD broadcast from a regular one?

In the catalog, such broadcasts are marked with an HD icon, and in the player you can see the available quality options.

Why is HD broadcast jerky?

Most likely, the connection speed is not enough. Lower the quality in your player, connect closer to your router, or close other downloads.

How much traffic does HD broadcasting consume?

Noticeably more than broadcast in standard quality. The exact volume depends on the resolution and bitrate, so on the mobile Internet it is better to choose a lower quality.

Is it necessary to watch HD on a big screen?

No, but on a laptop, monitor or TV the difference with normal quality is more noticeable than on a small phone screen.
