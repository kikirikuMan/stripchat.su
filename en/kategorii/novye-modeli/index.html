New models: why you should look

New models are women who have only recently started broadcasting on Stripchat. They are all at least 18 and have passed document verification, but webcam work is still new to them. Many viewers enjoy that.

The experienced model has thought out the lighting, the tip menu and even the jokes. The newcomer is still looking for his own style: he is embarrassed, surprised by the gifts, and rejoices at every viewer. Some regular visitors specifically come to this section to see how a person gets used to being on air.

What is the difference between beginner broadcasts

  • Spontaneity

    Beginners’ reactions have not yet been rehearsed. The model has not had time to get tired of the same type of questions and is happy about every conversation.

  • More attention

    There are only a few spectators in the room, so the model has time to respond to almost everyone who writes in the chat.

  • You influence the style

    Beginners often ask for advice: what light is better, what music to turn on, what to add to the broadcast. They really listen to the audience's answers.

  • You found her first

    It's nice to notice a model before she had thousands of fans and watch her grow.

How to find new models

The selection at the top shows broadcasts from the general feed. To see exactly the newcomers, use the Stripchat directory:

  1. Open the new models section or turn on a filter that shows recently registered girls.
  2. Add other parameters to it: language, appearance, air type. For example, you can search for new Russian models to communicate without a language barrier.
  3. Pay attention to the completeness of the profile: some already have a photo and description, while others only have a nickname. This happens often in the first days.
  4. If you like a model, immediately add it to your favorites: beginners’ schedules are often inconsistent.

Beginners are all different, but some things are repeated among them.

Getting acquainted

The model talks about herself, answers questions and asks where the audience is from. Now is the time to speak up: in a small room you will definitely be noticed.

Experiments with format

A newbie tries different things: today he dances, tomorrow he plays questions with the chat, then he launches a tip goal. Did you like something in particular? Write about it, the model will remember.

First private shows

Many beginners do not master private shows right away. If the model already accepts private shows, the conditions will be indicated in her profile. More details about the format are described in the guide about private shows.

Technical details

In the first broadcasts, something often doesn’t go according to plan: the camera is at the wrong angle, the music drowns out the voice, the light is too warm or too dim. Experienced models have figured this out a long time ago, and beginners tune in right in front of the audience. Many people like to watch how the picture becomes better from broadcast to broadcast, the room becomes more comfortable, and the model herself becomes more confident. And it’s quite possible to suggest something useful here.

How to support the new model

The first weeks are the hardest. The model does not yet know whether she will succeed, and a lot depends on how the audience receives her. Here's what helps:

  • Kind word. A simple “glad you're here” or a compliment to the image adds confidence.
  • Tip, even small ones. By tip the newcomer understands that the audience likes the broadcast.
  • Come back. When a model sees familiar nicknames, she understands that she has regular viewers, and her favorites will help her not to lose her.
  • If the camera is difficult to see or the sound is lost, politely report this. The model will be grateful.

What not to do

It’s easier to offend newbies, and after being rude in a chat, they may lose the desire to go on air for a long time. Therefore:

  • don’t take advantage of the model’s inexperience to put pressure on her and demand something she doesn’t offer;
  • don’t compare her to experienced models in a negative way;
  • do not ask about personal life, real name and city;
  • don’t laugh at technical mistakes, everyone started somewhere.

Frequently asked questions

How to understand that the model is new?

In the catalog, newcomers are usually marked with a separate label or collected in the section of new models. The tag lasts for a limited time after registration.

Are new models age verified?

Yes, just like all other models. Before going live, a model uses identity documents to confirm that she is at least 18 years old.

Why do newbies sometimes take a long time to respond in chat?

They are still getting used to the interface, light and camera. Give the model some time, and communication will improve.

Does it make sense to add a newcomer to your favorites?

Yes. The schedule of new models often varies, and a notification about the start of the broadcast will help you not to miss it.
