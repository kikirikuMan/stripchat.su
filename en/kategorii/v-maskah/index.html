The face is hidden, the character is visible

The masked webcam is live broadcast where the model hides a part of the face or the whole face behind the mask. In the frame, instead of the usual smile, lace, feathers, sequins or neon light, and the broadcast becomes like a little performance with a mystery in the center.

It is logical to assume that it is more difficult to communicate without a face. The masked model gets her voice, gestures, humor and attention to chat, and many viewers say that such models are remembered better: character remains in memory.

Why do models choose a mask

  • Anonymity

    The most common cause. The model conducts live broadcasts and is not afraid that her colleagues, neighbors or acquaintances will recognize it. In a mask, she is calmer, and a calm model and is freer.

  • Stage image

    For some, the mask works as an artist's pseudonym. Having put it on, the model becomes a character, for example, a mysterious stranger or comic book heroine.

  • Intrigue

    It is interesting for the viewer to guess emotions by the eyes and voice, and the models like to play with this interest.

  • Confidence

    It's easier for some beginners to start wearing a mask: less excitement in front of the camera, it's easier to get used to live broadcasts.

What are the masks

The choice is wider than you might think. Most commonly, live broadcasts are:

  • Venetian and carnival, with gold, feathers and painting. They are worn for solemn, theatrical shows.
  • Lace half masks: thin, almost weightless, cover only the eye area.
  • Glowing LED masks. A futuristic option that looks best in the twilight.
  • Cosplay masks and helmets for those who are released live in the image of their favorite character. There are many such models in the section cosplay.
  • animal masks (fox, cat, rabbit) for light and fun broadcasts.

Mask as a character

Many masked models have a thoughtful legend: name, character, manner of speech, favorite words. Regular viewers know this heroine and willingly play along, beginners are gradually drawn in. The character can change from live broadcast to live broadcast. The model selects the mask according to the mood, celebrates the “holidays” of her image, arranges themed evenings for the chat.

If you like broadcast-like broadcasts, there are more of these in live masks than in most other categories.

The mask remains in place

In an ordinary room, etiquette is reduced to courtesy. A model in a mask adds one strict rule to this: respect its decision not to show your face. It is impossible to buy it out for tips, this is the border that she has drawn consciously.

Don't try to figure out who the model is in real life. Do not compare it with photos from social networks, do not look for voice or interior details, do not share guesses in the chat. Even a joke like "I recognized you" can seriously scare a person.

What else to remember in such rooms:

  • Do not ask to remove the mask, neither for free, nor for tips;
  • Don't ask where the model lives, what her name is and where she works during the day;
  • Do not take screenshots and do not post them anywhere: this violates the rules of the site and the privacy of the model;
  • If someone in the chat is "investigating", do not pick up, the model or moderator will figure it out on their own.

General rules of conduct in rooms are collected in a guide on webcam etiquette.

how to watch such a live broadcast

Move your attention from face to everything else. Listen to the voice and intonation, follow the gestures, notice how the model reacts to the chat. Play along with her image: if she is a mysterious stranger, support the role and do not try to pull the “real” story out of it. The best conversations in such rooms happen when both sides play by the same rules.

Frequently asked questions

Why do webcam models masks?

Most often for the sake of anonymity: you can work in a mask without showing a face. For some, it's also a stage image.

Can I ask the model to remove the mask?

Not worth it. The model put it on consciously, and this is its border. Such requests, even for tips, are considered a gross violation of etiquette.

Do masks have communication and private shows like the rest?

Yes. This is the usual broadcast with chat, room goals, tips and private shows, only the image of the model differs.

How to find models in a mask?

Scroll through the preview and watch broadcast tags, the mask can be seen immediately. Save your favorite live broadcasts to favorites: such models usually do not change the image.
