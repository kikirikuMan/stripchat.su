Asia is big, and the countries there are different

Asian webcam chat is one of the most popular categories among viewers around the world. Although the word is short, the region behind it is huge: models air from Japan, Korea, China, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia and many other countries.

Each of these cultures has its own language and its own sense of humor, and they behave differently in front of the camera. So the first piece of advice is simple: don’t expect homogeneity from the category. A model from Manila is as different from a model from Tokyo as a girl from Madrid is from a girl from Helsinki. All models on the site are adults and confirm this with documents during registration.

The easiest way to search for Asian models is to filter by ethnic group or region in the girls' feed. Interested in a specific country? Add a language: Japanese, Korean, Thai or Tagalog. The filter takes data from the model's profile, so sometimes you have to scroll through the preview manually. Also consider the time of day. At the wrong time, the feed looks modest, although there are many models in the category.

How to communicate without a language barrier

Asian models almost never speak Russian, and many have working English, but not their native one. You can still reach an agreement if you keep a few things in mind:

  • Write in short sentences. "How is your day?" will understand immediately, but a long sentence with turns may not be understood.
  • Avoid slang and abbreviations, except for the most well-known ones.
  • With a translator, it is better to prepare a phrase in advance and insert a ready-made version. The literal translation from Russian is often clumsy.
  • Learn a greeting in the model's language. After "Konnichiwa", "annyeong" or "sawasdee" you will be noticed in the general flow.

Emojis and stickers are understandable to everyone, and they are especially readily installed in Asian rooms.

Tipping and privacy with a language barrier

Tipping is clear in any language: the model sees who supported her and usually thanks her by her nickname. But before the private show you should prepare a little. Chat first in the general chat and make sure that you and the model understand each other well. If her English is very basic, a conversational private conversation can turn into an exchange of emoticons, and the tokens will not go to what you wanted.

Before a private conversation, you should briefly write what you expect from him: to talk, watch a dance, or see a new image. One simple phrase in English is usually enough to avoid misunderstandings. How the tokens themselves work and what they are spent on is explained by the token guide.

Tact and respect

Beginners in this category often try to guess the nationality of the model out loud, and in vain. The question “Are you Chinese?” to a girl from Thailand sounds about the same as “are you from Poland?” to a man from Novosibirsk. If you are wondering where the model is from, look at the profile or ask neutrally: “Where are you streaming from?”

It is also better to keep stereotypes about appearance and character to yourself. Some models are shy, others are loud and daring, and nationality has nothing to do with it. Read more about how to behave in a chat in the etiquet guide.

Frequently asked questions

Most of all during the day, Moscow time: it is evening at this time. In the morning in Moscow the broadcasts are just starting, and at night in Asia it is early morning, and there is less choice.

What language do you use to communicate with an Asian model?

Usually in plain English. Write in short phrases without slang. Many models also understand basic phrases in Russian, but you shouldn’t count on it.

How to understand which country a model is from?

Look at the profile: region and languages are often indicated there. You should not guess the nationality out loud; it is better to ask politely if the model herself did not tell you.
