Solo shows: one model focusing on the chat

Home›Categories›Solo shows

one person in frame

webcam solo - the most classic live broadcast format: one in the frame The model, and the live broadcast is built around her and her communication with the audience. On the Stripchat, the solo shows form the basis of the girls' tape, so the choice is huge, from calm evening conversations to vigorous dance live broadcasts.

Why is the format so loved? When there is no one in the room except the model, she has no one to distract with. She looks at the camera, speaks with a chat, and answers your message personally to you. In a show where there are several people in the frame, it is more difficult to achieve such a contact.

What is the solo-direct Broadcast

The style of each model is different, but most of the solo broadcasts go through similar stages. If you know them, it's easier to understand at what point in a live broadcast you got.

  1. Start. The model greets regular viewers, tells how the day went, adjusts the light and music. It's time to get acquainted.
  2. goals of the room. The amount of tokens appears on the screen, after which the model will make the promised: dance, change the image or start the game.
  3. The height of the live broadcast. Chat comes to life, tips go more often, the model responds to them with sounds, gestures or jokes.
  4. private show or ending. Some of the viewers call the model to a private show, or she finishes the public part and says goodbye until the next time.

Those who want to immediately see the reaction to each token are suitable models With interactive toys: in the solo-broadcast, the connection between the viewer and the model is especially noticeable here.

solo, pair or group: what's the difference

  • solo

    one model, and it sets the pace. The show depends on her mood and how she feels her chat. You communicate directly with her.

  • pair

    two in the frame, and a lot happens between them, and the viewer watches the duet more. Learn more about the format on the page show couple.

  • Group

    participants three or more, all the time Something happens, but the turn rarely reaches the individual viewer. It looks like a small party.

Solo is usually chosen by those who want to chat. You don't get bored at the same time: the experienced model keeps the room for hours, changing themes, music and images.

What are the solo shows

translations inside the format are very different. The most common are:

  • colloquial. The model mostly communicates: she discusses films and trips, answers questions.
  • dance, with music and change of outfits. Fans of this should look into the dance live broadcasts.
  • game. Wheel of fortune, riddles, tasks from viewers where chat participates directly.
  • thematic. The model chooses an image for the evening, such as cosplay, retro or business suit, plays a role, and the chat plays along.
  • slow. subdued light, calm music, no fuss. Such live broadcasts often go late at night.

How to find your solo model

The tape of solo broadcasts is huge, and the first previews rarely turn out to be "the same". In order not to scroll without end, try to act like this.

First, decide what you want from the evening: talk, watch dance or play. Then open two or three rooms and sit in each for a few minutes in silence. Note how the model communicates with other viewers. If she calls people by nicknames, jokes and remembers what she was talking about, it will be nice to talk to her. The profile description often indicates the languages, schedule and rules of the room.

Add to favorite models, then it will be visible when they are online again. Details on how to choose is written in the guide How to choose a model.

The model in the solo-broadcast itself sets the boundaries of the show. If the profile says what it doesn't do, don't ask for it either in the chat or for tips.

A couple of tips for pleasant communication

In the solo room, the model sees the entire chat at once, and how you behave is noticeable to it. Hello when you come in, such a trifle is appreciated. Write briefly so that you can read the message in between. Do not repeat the same request many times. If the model didn't answer, it's most likely she's just busy with the show.

The solo model works alone and in its live broadcast is its own host, operator and sound engineer. Be a little more patient and polite than the others, and in her room you will soon be welcome.

Frequently asked questions

what does "solo" mean in webcam?

This is the name of the broadcast, where there is only one model in front of the camera. She herself conducts a live broadcast, communicates with the chat and decides what the show will be like.

You can watch the solo show for free?

public live broadcast is open to everyone without payment. Tokens are needed if you want to support the model tips, help close the room's target or call it to a private show.

Why doesn't one model respond right away?

She plays the show at the same time, monitors the camera and reads the messages of many viewers. In the busy room of the answer, sometimes you have to wait a bit.

Why is a solo live broadcast more convenient for a beginner?

it's easier to figure out how webcam works. There is one person in the frame, the rules are written in the profile, and the model responds to messages herself.
