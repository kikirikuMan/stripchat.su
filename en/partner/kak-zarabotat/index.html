Earning with the Stripchat affiliate program

Where does a partner's income come from?

There is only one way to earn in Stripcash: to bring to Stripchat users who register and start using paid functions. The platform shares with the partner part of the revenue or pays for a certain action. The site, channel, advertising campaigns are needed exactly in order to find such users.

Keep in mind that you can watch broadcasts for free, so the click alone is worth nothing. Money appears when the person you bring buys tokens. It turns out that the number of transitions decides less than the willingness of your audience to pay. If you are not yet familiar with the program as a whole, start with affiliate review.

payment models: how they are arranged

Adult affiliate programs usually use two common payment models. Your Stripcash dashboard shows which are available to you and on what terms. Here, we explain only the general principle.

Revenue Share

The partner receives a percentage of what the given user spends on the service. If a person stays on the platform for a long time and regularly buys tokens, accruals all this time are under the terms of the program. This income is growing slowly. In the first weeks after launch, the amount is usually modest, and then a lot depends on the behavior of the audience, which you do not directly affect.

The scheme is well suited for SEO sites and channels that have been working for years and bringing loyal visitors.

Action Payment (Pay Per Sale, CPA)

The partner receives a fixed amount for a specific action, such as the first purchase of the given user. Money comes faster and it's easier to plan it: you know how much one conversion costs, and you can compare it to the price of a click on an ad network. But all the subsequent expenses of this user will not bring you anything.

This model is convenient for media buyers who buy traffic and want to quickly understand if the campaign pays off.

What affects income

  • Traffic quality

    The person who was looking for webcam chat and read your review is converted much better than a casual visitor with popup ads. Interest in the topic here weighs more volume.

  • GEO

    Audience's payability and available payment methods vary widely across countries. Similar traffic from different regions can give a very different result.

  • Conversion

    Share of visitors who registered and made a purchase. It is influenced by the format of the promo, location, site speed and how much the promise coincides with what a person sees after a click.

  • Device

    Mobile and desktop traffic behave differently. Check how your website and selected promotional materials look on your phone.

  • Hold

    If the user shares the revenue, it is important if the user returns. When the presentation is honest, the person is more willing to remain.

How to calculate the result

Apart from the numbers, one can only guess. Here is the minimum set of indicators that should be done from the first day.

  1. Visitors for each source: site, channel, advertising campaign.
  2. Clicks on affiliate links and widgets.
  3. registration and first purchases from statistics Stripcash.
  4. Accruals for the period and if you buy traffic, the cost of it.

Separate the sources using sub-accounts or labels, otherwise you won't understand which one brings money and which one just spends the budget. Learn more about tags and statistics in the section about Partner Tools.

To compare sources, the most convenient income per thousand visitors or one hundred clicks, it takes into account both the conversion and the quality of the audience. If you buy traffic, match this number with the price of the same thousand impressions or clicks. When expenses are consistently higher than the charges, it's time to change or stop the campaign. "Twisting" it in the hope of a miracle is usually useless.

Example of calculation logic

Let's say you have two sources: a review article on the site and a post in a thematic channel. On transitions, the channel wins, but in the statistics Stripcash it can be seen that there are more registrations and purchases from the article. It seems that review readers come with a more conscious interest. It is logical to write more detailed materials, and in the channel change the feed and try other formats. Figures in such a comparison are taken only from your statistics, there are no common “market averages” for this.

Evaluate the result over a long period, the first days say little. On a small amount of data, everything is decided by case: one purchase makes a week “successful”, and without it, the same week looks “failed”.

Realistic expectations

Affiliate income is not guaranteed. Many novice webmasters have been working at zero for months, while the site is gaining positions or until a working link in advertising is found. Paid purchase without numbers control easily goes down. It happens to many, and this is not a deception.

Third-party sites promising a specific income "from day one" usually make mistakes or exaggerate. Focus on the conditions in your office and your own statistics.

To avoid repeating other people's mistakes, read the analysis errors of beginners. When the first accruals appear, figure out how they are arranged Partner payments.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does the partner get Stripcash?

Specific rates depend on the payment model, GEO and the program conditions at the moment. They are indicated in the personal account Stripcash, and on third-party sites may be obsolete.

What is more profitable: Revenue Share or payment per action?

Depends on how you work. The share of spending is good for a loyal audience and long work, and a one-time payment for an action is more convenient when purchasing traffic, when expenses need to be recaptured quickly.

Is it possible to earn money?

There are no guarantees here. The partner's income depends on the quality and volume of traffic, geo and conversion, and in the first months it is quite possible to remain at zero.

How much traffic does it take to make income?

There is no threshold. A hundred targeted visitors from a suitable geo sometimes brings more than a thousand random clicks, so look at your statistics.
