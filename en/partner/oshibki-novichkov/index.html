Mistakes new affiliates make and how to avoid them

Why do beginners waste time

The mistakes of beginners in the affiliate Stripcash are surprisingly similar to each other. The program pays, the niche is alive, but the projects are still closed because of the same mistakes: someone is trying to cut off the path, someone does not count the numbers, someone expects something that does not happen. Below are eight such errors and what to do instead of them.

1. Thin and duplicated pages

Script is familiar. A person makes a website and generates a hundred pages for requests of the type “webs + city” or “webkam + category”, and the text is the same everywhere, only the keyword changes. After a couple of months, the search engines understand that the pages are no different, and the entire site goes under the filter, including normal pages.

It is better to make fewer pages, but each should have its own meaning. If there is nothing to fill the page with, except for replacing one word, it is not needed. One detailed guide brings more than twenty pacifiers. How to set up a small quality site, see about the site on GitHub Pages.

2. Spam instead of promotion

links in other people's chats, comments below each post, mass private messages. From the outside, it looks like a quick way to get transitions. In fact, accounts are blocked, complaints accumulate, and annoyed people buy almost nothing. In addition, spam violates the rules of the affiliate program, and you can lose accruals for it.

Build your own sites, whether it's a website, a channel or a community, and bring people there who came for the topic themselves.

3. Buying garbage traffic

Cheap clicks of "packs", dubious exchangers, offers like "ten thousand visitors for a penny." Such traffic consists entirely of bots or people who were paid for the transition. There will be no registrations or purchases from him, and the program may well consider it a fraud.

Buy traffic only from established advertising networks that officially accept 18+ topics, and start with a small budget. Read more in the article about Traffic Sources.

4. Ignore platform rules and law

Webcam advertising on general-purpose social networks, disguised links, and promotion where the audience may include minors. Beginners may think that “everyone does it” and nobody will notice. Violations are noticed, often after a channel or account has already built an audience.

Before launching, read the rules of the site, the conditions Stripcash and the requirements of the legislation of your country. If the topic is banned, look for another site. Workarounds don't work here.

5. Work without analytics

One link to everything at once: site, channel, advertising. A month later, there are some registrations in the statistics, but it is not clear where they come from. What to develop, the beginner does not know and distributes forces blindly.

  • Enter a separate label for each source and each ad campaign.
  • Put the attendance counter on the site to see which pages are reading.
  • Once a week, make a table of visitors, clicks, registration and accrual.

How Tags and Statistics Work are described in Partner Tools.

6. Audience Deception

Clickbait headlines, promises of what is not on the service, fictional prices and “secret free private shows”. A person calls, sees something completely different and leaves. Trust in the site or channel drops, and with it the conversion of all future publications.

Write the truth, mention the cons and do not invent numbers. An honest review lives longer than the advertising text. If you are not sure about some fact, for example, in the price of tokens or payment methods, invite the reader to check it on the service itself. It's better than writing what will turn out to be wrong in a month. Outdated and fictional data undermine people's trust and spoil the attitude of search engines to the site.

7. Too early failure

A month after launching the SEO site, it almost always looks like a failure, because the search engines have just started indexing it. The first advertising campaign often goes into the red until the formats and GEO are selected. Many throw just at this moment, without waiting for data on which you can solve something.

Determine how much time and money you are willing to invest in the test, and summarize when this period ends. The first days don't talk about anything. Refusing a non-working source is normal, just the solution should rely on statistics, emotions are a bad adviser here.

8. Wrong expectations

Affiliate marketing is often sold as "passive income". In practice, this is work: you need to write texts, follow the rules, parse the numbers and try new approaches. income not guaranteed, and in some projects it remains zero. Anyone who is ready for this decides more calmly and rarely falls into the seven traps described above.

what the partner's income actually consists of is described in the article how to make money in Stripcash. Look at specific bets in the office, you should not believe advertising promises here.

short start-up checklist

  1. Read the rules Stripcash and the sites where you will work.
  2. Choose one traffic source that you already know how to handle.
  3. Make some useful unique pages or publications.
  4. Mark links with tags from day one.
  5. Evaluate the result after a pre-selected period.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common beginner's mistake in affiliate room?

Most often, this is work without analytics plus high expectations. The beginner does not see what brings the result, and quits the project without waiting for statistics.

Is it possible to make many pages for different queries with the same text?

not worth it. Search engines consider such pages as duplicates or doorways and lower the entire site. Each page should have its own question and its own text.

How long does it take to wait for the first results?

strongly depends on the source. Paid advertising brings clicks at once, although purchases are not guaranteed, and the SEO site usually needs months to go to the search.

Why can a partner account be blocked?

Usually for prohibited traffic: spam, bots, motivated clicks or user scams. A complete list of violations is in the program rules.
