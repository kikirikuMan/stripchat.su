Promotional tools for Stripcash affiliates

Why does a partner need multiple tools?

All tools Stripcash solve one problem: to make your visitor become a user of Stripchat and the system writes it down for you. For a post in the channel, one format is convenient, for a site with another category, another for an advertising campaign. Below are the main types of promos that are in the program, and where each of them is appropriate.

A specific set of formats, their settings and codes are issued in the personal account. The names and interface change over time, so the principle of operation is described here, without binding to the buttons. How tools are related to payment models, read the article how to make money in Stripcash.

Referral links

Everyone starts with them. Your identifier is sewn in the link, and the visitor that has passed on it is assigned to you. Usually you can choose where it leads: to the main page, to the registration page or to a specific category of broadcasts.

It's easy to mess up the link, and most often trifles. Address abbreviations and extra redirects sometimes cut off the parameters, and the transition does not count. If you need a short address, make a test transition and check it in the statistics. You should not disguise a link as something else either, the visitor must understand what will get to the webcam service.

You can put links to the buttons of the site, in the description of the Telegram channel, in the text of the review. On this site, for example, all registration buttons lead to regular affiliate links. Send the visitor to where he will immediately see the promise. The link to the desired category almost always works better than to the general main one.

Banners

Ready-made pictures of different sizes with a built-in referral link. They will be useful on sites with advertising spaces and when purchasing in adult networks. Inserting a banner is as easy as shelling pears, however, the banner blindness has not gone away: visitors are used to sliding their eyes past the ad units. Best of all, the banner works in tandem with the text and does not do well alone.

Before placing, make sure the selected banner fits your site's rules. Many sites and networks accept only versions without explicit images.

Embedded broadcasts (Iframe and creatives)

The most visual format. On your page there are live broadcasts that are updated themselves, the visitor sees what is happening right now, and switches to the service, already choosing the broadcast. This is how the main page of this site is arranged, the broadcasts on it are built through the program widget.

  • Filters

    You can usually set a broadcast category to show what matches the page theme.

  • Appearance

    The size and design are customizable so the widget can be entered into the website design.

  • Safe Mode

    If there is an option without explicit previews in the settings, it will come in handy for areas with restrictions.

The widget looks good on pages of categories and on the main page. At the same time, it does not give unique content, and search engines are not interested in a page from one iframe, so add useful text to it. A step-by-step example is in the guide How to make a website on GitHub Pages.

Landing pages and landing pages

Promo pages that the program itself prepares and Sharpens for registration. They are used mainly by media buyers: the ad in the advertising network leads to the landing page with your identifier, and the landing page brings the visitor to the creation of an account. Try several options, on the same traffic, landings sometimes noticeably differ in conversion.

Test honestly: one landing page vs another on the same source, geo and devices, each has its own label. It is useless to compare the results collected in different weeks and from different sites, because the difference can give traffic, and the page will have nothing to do with it.

White label

A full webcam site under your brand and domain based platform. Users register and buy on your site, while broadcasts, payments and support remain with the service. In fact, this is a separate project with its own advantages and responsibilities, and you can’t call it a link. For more format, see the article What is White Label.

Statistics and sub-ID

The statistics show transitions, registrations, purchases and accruals. It will be much more useful if you add labels (sub-id) to links. Here's what the markup of a small project might look like.

in a couple of weeks, this markup will show which pages and sources bring paying users. Without marks, you have only the total amount, and it is not clear what to scale.

API

For those who are friends with the code, the program opens program access to data. In general terms, through the API, you can receive broadcast lists and arrange them on the site in your own way, as well as pick up statistics in your own analytics systems. This helps out when there is not enough standard widget, for example, you need your own catalog layout or automatic reports. Documentation and access keys are in the office. If you do not write the code, start with links and widgets, most projects have enough of them with a margin.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest StripCash tool?

Regular referral link. It is put in the text, on the button or in the description of the channel, without setting anything.

Is it possible to embed broadcasts on your site?

possible. The program has widgets with live broadcasts that are inserted through the iframe, and the code and settings are in the partner's office.

Why do you need a sub-id?

With tags, statistics are shared by sources, pages and campaigns. Without them, it remains to guess what exactly brings the registration.

Do I need a programmer to work with an API?

API requires basic development skills. Links, banners and widgets do without a programmer: it is enough to insert the finished code onto the page.
