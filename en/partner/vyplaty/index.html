Payouts for Stripcash affiliates

How payments are arranged in Stripcash

Payouts Stripcash close the entire chain. The users you provided do what the program takes into account, accruals appear on the balance sheet, and when the amount reaches the threshold, it is transferred to you in the chosen way. It looks simple, but just here, beginners have the most questions: where is the money, why they didn’t come, what to write in the details.

I will make a reservation right away: this site is not an official resource Stripcash. There are no specific ways, minimum amounts, commissions and payment dates here, because they are set by the program, they differ in methods and change from time to time. You should only believe in the payment section in your personal account. If you haven't figured out where the charges come from yet, start with an article About payment models.

Money path: from click to your account

  1. The visitor clicks on your link or widget and registers on StripChat.
  2. He performs actions for which the program charges a reward, such as buying tokens.
  3. Accrual appears in the statistics. Some operations are checked for some time, affiliate programs are thus protected from fraud.
  4. The confirmed amount is accumulated on the partner's balance sheet.
  5. At the end of the billing period, if the balance is above the threshold and the details are filled out, a payment is formed.
  6. the money comes in the chosen way. How much they go depends on the method and payment system of the recipient.

Output methods and minimum threshold

Adult affiliate programs usually offer several kinds of payment methods: bank transfers, electronic payment services and other options available in particular countries. Your Stripcash dashboard lists the available methods and the minimum amount for each.

When choosing a method, compare several parameters.

  • Minimum amount. The threshold is different for different methods. As long as there is little traffic, the low threshold method will allow you to get the first money earlier.
  • Commission. In addition to the program commission, if any, consider the fees of your bank or wallet for incoming transfer and currency conversion.
  • Availability in your country. Not every method works in every jurisdiction, and some banks are wary of payments from adult entertainment companies.
  • Enrollment speed. Transfers between banks of different countries sometimes go longer than electronic payments.

How often do payments come

In different affiliate programs, payments are different, somewhere in short cycles, about once a month. See the graph Stripcash in the program conditions. And keep a couple of moments in your head.

  • If the balance has not reached the threshold by the end of the period, the sum usually passes to the next period and continues to accumulate. The money doesn't go away, they just come later.
  • the payment is formed in one day, and at the expense it comes to another. Between these dates, the payment system processes it.

At the start, while there is little traffic, payments are irregular: in one period, the amount will exceed the threshold, in the other it is not. The charges are not lost from this. Look at the balance in the office, and if it grows, everything is in order. Plan your budget for advertising or domain renewal from already received payments. Accruals that are still on the test are not suitable for this.

Taxes and Accounting

Partnership Remuneration is considered income, and in most countries it is paid tax. The order depends on the jurisdiction and your status: an individual, self-employed, individual entrepreneur or company. There is no single recipe, there are only general tips.

  • Save the payment history and account statements, they will be needed for reporting.
  • Record project costs: domain, hosting, advertising. In some modes, they can be deducted.
  • If money comes from abroad, deal with currency control.
  • When the amounts become noticeable, go to an accountant or tax consultant in your country.
This section is not a tax or legal advice. The rules change, and the recipient himself is responsible for the declaration of income.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum withdrawal amount in Stripcash?

The threshold depends on the payment method and the conditions of the program. Look for the current figure in the payments section of the Personal Account.

How often does Stripcash pay money to partners?

The frequency of the program determines the conditions of the program, and for different ways of output it may differ. The graph is more reliable to look at in the office, third-party sites often lag behind reality.

Why didn't the accruals arrive on time?

Usually the amount did not reach the threshold, the details are not filled out or filled out with an error, or the accruals are still checked. If everything is in order with this, write to the program support.

Do I need to pay taxes on affiliate income?

As a rule, you need to: income from affiliate programs is taxed according to the laws of your country. The order depends on the status and jurisdiction, it is better to check with the tax consultant.
