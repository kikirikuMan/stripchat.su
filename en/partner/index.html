What is Stripcash

Affiliate program Stripcash is the official affiliate of the webcam service Stripchat. A webmaster, channel owner, or arbitrager brings new users to Stripchat via their referral link and receives rewards for their activity. Responsibilities are divided simply: you traffic, from the broadcast platform, payments and user support.

This site and the affiliate program itself, broadcasts on the main one are embedded through the program tools. Therefore, practical experience is collected here, without advertising promises. If you want to understand where the partner's income comes from, start by parsing payment models.

How referral tracking works

  1. After registration you get Personal links and promotional materials with their ID.
  2. The visitor follows the link, banner or widget, and the system remembers that it came from you.
  3. If the user registers and does what the program takes into account, it falls into your statistics.
  4. Accruals are accumulated on the balance sheet and withdrawn according to the rules from on payments.

Where to start a partner

There is no need to build a big project from the first day. First, choose one channel that you already know how to conduct, and look at it how the audience reacts. The site owner is easiest to add a thematic section with built-in broadcasts. For those who write texts, a small static site is suitable, and how to make it for free is described in the guide Website on GitHub Pages.

Immediately get a simple table: where the visitors came from, how many clicks there were and how many registrations were shown by the statistics Stripcash. In a few weeks, it will become clear which source to develop and which to close. Do not promise the audience what is not on the platform, and follow the rules of each site where you put links. If the channel is blocked or the site falls under the filter, all work will go down the drain.

Read the conditions of the program in your personal account, retellings on the forums are not suitable for this. The rates, rules and list of prohibited traffic sources sets Stripcash, and it is its conditions that apply to your charges.
