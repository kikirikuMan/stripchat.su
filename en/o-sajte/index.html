About Stripchat.su

What this site is

Stripchat.su is an independent information site about Stripchat webcam streams. It brings together live broadcasts, clear guides for viewers and a straightforward explanation of how webcam modeling and the affiliate program work.

It is not the official Stripchat website, and its only connection to the company is through the affiliate program. The site does not operate the platform, process payments or store account details.

Where the broadcasts come from

The broadcast section on our pages is an official affiliate program widget. It loads directly from the platform's servers and shows models who are currently live. The platform handles everything inside the widget, including registration, chat and token purchases, under its own rules.

Widget previews are configured to avoid showing explicit content. The platform verifies models' ages using identity documents, and only adults can broadcast.

How the site makes money

Stripchat.su participates in the affiliate program. If you register on the platform through a link on this site and make a purchase, the site receives a percentage of that purchase. You pay the same amount as you would without the link. Registration links are marked as sponsored.

This keeps the site free to use, with no banners or third-party advertising scripts.

Site rules

  • Adults only: 18+. This site is intended for adults. Content involving minors is not and will never be included.
  • No piracy. The site does not publish or promote recordings or leaks of other people's broadcasts because doing so violates models' rights.
  • No invented figures. Our articles do not promise guaranteed income or use fabricated statistics. When conditions depend on the platform, we state that directly.
  • Respectful language. Articles explain how things work without explicit descriptions.

What you can find here

Viewers can browse broadcast categories and guides on watching for free, understanding tokens and protecting their privacy. People considering webcam work can visit the model section, while website and channel owners can explore the affiliate program section.

Our privacy policy explains what data is processed when you visit the site. Limitations of liability are described in the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stripchat.su the official Stripchat website?

No. It is an independent affiliate site. Streams are embedded through the official affiliate widget, while the platform itself handles registration and payments.

How does the site make money?

Through the Stripchat affiliate program. When a visitor registers through a link here and makes a purchase on the platform, the site earns a commission. The price for the visitor stays the same.

Who should I contact about my account or a payment?

Contact Stripchat support. The platform itself manages accounts, tokens and payments; Stripchat.su has no access to them.
