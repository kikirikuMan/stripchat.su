Privacy policy

Overview

Stripchat.su is a static website. It has no forms, registration or comments, collects no personal data and sets no cookies of its own.

Third-party services used on the site's pages may process data. These include the hosting provider, the broadcast widget and analytics systems, if enabled. The sections below explain what data is involved and where to find more information.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, the hosting service receives technical request data: your IP address, browser type, page address and the time of the request. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy policy. The site owner has no access to the server logs.

Broadcast widget

The broadcast section loads from the Stripchat affiliate program's servers and follows the platform's rules. The platform may set its own cookies, track visits through affiliate links and process information you enter when registering or making a payment. This data goes to the platform; Stripchat.su does not receive it.

If you register on the platform or buy tokens, read its privacy policy and terms of use.

Affiliate links

Registration links for viewers, models and affiliates contain the identifier of this site's affiliate account. This tells the platform that you arrived from this site. The site owner receives no personal data; affiliate statistics show only anonymized registrations and purchases.

Analytics

If web analytics systems such as Yandex Metrica or Google Analytics are enabled, they collect anonymized visit statistics: which pages people open, how long they spend on them and which devices they use. This information helps improve the site and is not used to identify individuals. You can disable collection in your browser settings or with a tracker blocker.

What you can do

  • Clear cookies and site data in your browser settings.
  • Block third-party cookies, although this may affect how the broadcast widget works.
  • Open the site in incognito mode so visits are not saved in your browser history.

Our guide to anonymity explains how to protect your privacy on the platform itself.

Policy changes

This page will be updated if the site adds services that process data. General information about the site is available on the About page.
