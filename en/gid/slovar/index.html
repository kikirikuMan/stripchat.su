A webcam glossary for viewers

Why do you need a dictionary

In webcam chat a beginner is most confused by the following words: “type”, “goal”, “spare”, “C2C”, “loves”. Almost all of them came from English and took root in Russian-speaking rooms, without translation. This webcam dictionary explains basic concepts in simple terms so you can understand what the model and other viewers are talking about.

Functions may be named differently on different platforms, and terms and conditions change over time. Therefore, general definitions are given here, and see the exact rules on the site itself.

Money and payment

TermWhat it means
TokensInternal currency of the site. They are bought in packages and spent on tips, private shows and other paid functions. More details in the guide about tokens.
Type, tipsTokens that the viewer sends to the model right during the broadcast. The word comes from the English tip, hence the verb “to tip.”
Tip menuList of model actions with prices in tokens: answer a question, dance, change image, and so on. Sent the amount from the menu, and the model executes this item.
GoalThe goal of the ether, that is, the amount of tokens, after collecting which the model shows the promised show. Any viewer can chip in, everyone can see the progress. How to work with the menu and goals is described in the guide about tipping.
TipperA viewer who sends a tip. The most generous models are often noted in the chat.

Types of shows

TermWhat it means
Public broadcastRegular broadcast, open to everyone. You can watch for free, and write to the chat after registration.
PrivateOne-on-one show with the model. Payment is per minute, in tokens, and other viewers do not see the conversation. Details in the guide about private shows.
Exclusive private showA private show option that cannot be viewed in spy mode, so only you can see the show. It usually costs more than a standard private show.
Spy modeThe ability to view someone else’s private show for tokens, without having the right to write in the chat. Works only if the private model and format allow it.
Group showA performance for which several spectators pay at once. It costs everyone less than personal private show, and the model performs for the entire group.
Ticket showTicket show. The viewer pays a fixed amount in advance and gets access to the performance at the appointed time.

Technology and interactive

TermWhat it means
Cum-tu-cam, C2CMode in which the model sees you through your camera. Most often it is available in a private show and is turned on only if you want it.
Lavens, lovensThis is how interactive toys are called in conversation, named after the famous manufacturer. They are connected to the ether, and they respond to tips: more tokens, stronger or longer reaction. Such broadcasts are collected in the category interactive toys.

Account and community

TermWhat it means
FavoritesYour personal list of favorite models. It makes them easy to find and you can immediately see who is on air now.
Fan ClubPaid subscription for one specific model. As a rule, it gives access to restricted materials, a special icon in the chat and other bonuses that the model chooses herself.
Ultimate, premium subscriptionPaid subscription for the entire site, without reference to a separate model. Typically unlocks advanced features and privileges in chat, the exact set varies by platform.
Moderator, modA viewer whom the model entrusted to keep order in the chat. It can delete messages and restrict violators.
GuestViewer without an account or without purchased tokens. He can watch the broadcast, but in the chat his capabilities are limited.
DM, PMPrivate messages from the model, bypassing the general chat. Many services are not available to every user.

Model statuses

The broadcast preview and in the room itself often have a marker that makes it clear what is happening now. Signatures vary, but usually mean the following.

  • “Online”, “on air”: there is a public broadcast, come and watch.
  • “Private”, “group”: the model is busy with a paid show, and the public broadcast is still closed.
  • “Moved away”, “break”: the model briefly left the frame, the broadcast will continue soon.
  • “Offline”: no broadcast at the moment. If a model is in your favorites, you will know when it returns.
  • “New Model”: She recently started working on the platform. Viewers are usually happy with such newcomers.

Short phrases from chat

  • “In private” means that the model is busy with a private show and the public broadcast is paused.
  • “Goal is closed”: the amount has been collected, now the promised will begin.
  • “BRB” is written when the model moves away for a short time.
  • "PVT?" They ask if they can call the model privately.
  • Models write “Ty” (thank you) in gratitude for the tip.
  • “Np” stands for no problem, that is, “no problem” or “no problem.”
  • “Hi all”, “Hey guys”: this is how the model greets everyone who enters the room.
  • “Lurker” is a viewer who watches silently and does not write in the chat. There is nothing wrong with this, many people do this.
  • “Regular” means a regular viewer who often looks into the room. Models recognize such people and greet them by their nickname.
  • “Ban” and “kick” mean blocking a viewer from a room or removing it from it for violating the rules. How to avoid this is described in the guide on chat etiquette.
Meet an unfamiliar word in the chat? Politely ask the model or moderator. The question is quite common, and newcomers to the rooms are usually treated kindly.

With these words you can calmly enter any room and understand what is happening there.

Frequently asked questions

How is a private show different from a group show?

In private, the model communicates only with you. A group show is paid for by several spectators at once, so it’s cheaper for everyone.

What does “model in private” mean?

The model is currently hosting a private show with another viewer, and the public broadcast has been stopped for the time being. If she has enabled spy mode, you can sometimes spy on the show.

Why are tokens needed and not regular money?

Tokens are the internal currency of the platform. They make it easier to make small payments like tips, and you don't have to enter card details every time you make a transaction.

What does “type” mean in chat?

This is what they call a tip, from the English tip. Accordingly, “tip” means sending tokens to the model.
