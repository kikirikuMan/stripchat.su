How to register on Stripchat

What to prepare in advance

Registration on Stripchat is free and takes a couple of minutes. A couple of things should still be decided before pressing the button: which email to register with, which nickname to use and which password to set. It’s easier to choose right away than to change later.

  • Mail. Many people keep a separate mailbox for entertainment sites so that emails from the webcam platform do not end up in the same thread as work and family ones.
  • Password. It must be new, never used anywhere else. If you have a password manager, trust it to generate it.
  • Nickname. There is a separate section about it below: models and other viewers in the chat will recognize you by your nickname.

Registration step by step

The platform interface is sometimes updated, and the labels on the buttons may differ slightly. The procedure remains the same:

  1. Go to Stripchat and find the sign up button. As a rule, it appears at the top of the page and also pops up when a guest tries to write in the chat or add the model to favorites.
  2. Confirm that you are at least 18 years old. Otherwise, the platform will not let you continue.
  3. Enter a nickname. If it is taken, the system will offer to come up with another one.
  4. Enter your email and create a password.
  5. Read the terms of use and privacy policy. If you are satisfied with everything, complete the registration.
  6. Go to your email and confirm your address using the link in the email.

Sometimes the platform offers to log in through a third-party service. This is faster, but then the webcam account will be linked to your main profile. If privacy is important to you, register in the usual way, via email. For more tips on this topic, see on anonymity.

How to choose a nickname

When you write to the chat, the first thing the model sees is your nickname. It determines what impression you make and how secure your personal information is. Here's what you should pay attention to.

  • No personal information

    Do not put your first and last name, year of birth, city or company name in your nickname. The nickname by which you are known on social networks or games is also not suitable.

  • Easy to read

    Models often address the audience out loud. “NightOwl” or “Sever” are easy to pronounce, but with something like “xX_k1ll3r_2k_Xx” you will have to suffer.

  • No rudeness

    Vulgar and provocative nicknames of models are annoying, and in some rooms such viewers are immediately blocked.

  • For a long time

    Models remember regular viewers by their nickname. If you keep coming back to the same people, the name will gradually become part of your familiarity.

A simple way: take a word that is associated with a hobby or mood, and add something neutral to it. For example, "TeaLover", "QuietEvening" or "Orion". The Cyrillic alphabet is not always accepted in nicknames, so for a Russian name, come up with a Latin backup option in advance.

Email confirmation

Immediately after registration you will receive an email with a link. Until you click on it, some of the account functions may not work, and without a verified address, you will not be able to recover your password. If there is no letter for several minutes, do this:

  1. Look in the Spam, Promotions, and Mailings folders. Email providers love to hide letters from entertainment sites there.
  2. In your account settings, check whether the address is entered correctly. Most often it’s a matter of one extra or missing letter.
  3. Ask to send the letter again. This button is usually found on your profile or in a pop-up reminder.
  4. If this does not work, write to platform support using the form on the website.

Typical registration errors

  • Registration on a fake site. There are pages that copy the design of webcam platforms and collect passwords. Before entering data, look at the address in the browser bar.
  • Old password from email or social network. If a database leaks one day, this password will be tried on all sites in a row. There is no such problem with a unique password.
  • Work email. Emails from a cam site in a corporate inbox can easily turn into awkward questions.
  • Nick thought up in a second. It’s a shame to show a random set of letters in a chat, and you may not be able to change it.
  • Unverified email. If you forget your password, it is more difficult to recover such an account.

If you are not yet sure that you need an account at all, compare the options in the guide about viewing without registration.

What to do immediately after registration

Open your profile settings. There, unnecessary mailings and notifications are disabled, the interface language is selected and privacy is configured. Then add to your favorites a couple of models that caught your eye while you were watching as a guest. The site will let you know when they go live.

Separately, it is worth securing your login. If the platform offers additional confirmation upon login, turn it on. It will take a minute, and it will become much more difficult to take away an account with a balance of tokens. Store your password in a password manager, not in notes on your phone, and don’t log into your account from other people’s devices. If you had to sign in on someone else’s device, remember to sign out and close your browser. If it seems that someone has gained access, immediately change your password and look at your purchase history.

Before your first chat message, it's a good idea to review the etiquette rules to ensure you make a good impression from the start. If you want to support the model, you will need instructions on how to buy tokens calmly and within your budget.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to register on Stripchat?

Not at all, it's free. You will only have to spend money if you want to buy tokens yourself.

Can I change my nickname later?

This depends on the current platform rules and may be limited. Therefore, it is better to immediately choose a name that you won’t get tired of.

The confirmation email did not arrive. What to do?

Look at “Spam” and “Promotions”, check the address for typos and request the letter again in your account settings. If it doesn’t help, write to platform support.

Do I need to indicate my real name?

An ordinary viewer will only need a nickname, email and password. It is better not to show your real name in your nickname.
