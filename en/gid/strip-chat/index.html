What the platform can do

The set of functions changes over time, so below is only the general picture, without specific numbers.

    Most of the broadcasts are open: the video is on, the chat can be read, the model communicates with the entire room. What exactly is available for free is described in detail in the guide about free viewing.

  • Tokens

    Internal currency of the site. Tokens are used to thank the model and pay for menu items and private shows. See prices and packages on the platform itself.

  • Private shows

    One-on-one communication with the model for a fee. The model sets the conditions, and she also decides whether to accept the invitation.

  • Filters and categories

    The catalog is filtered by language, type of broadcast, appearance, country and a number of other characteristics. All models on the platform are adults and undergo age verification.

  • Mobile version

    The site is adapted for the phone browser. The player and chat fit on the screen, and the catalog is convenient to flip through with your finger.

  • VR and interactive toys

    There are broadcasts for VR helmets and broadcasts with devices that respond to tips. This is a nice addition, although it cannot be called the basis of the platform.
