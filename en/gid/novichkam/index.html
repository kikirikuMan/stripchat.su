Guide for beginners: how Stripchat works

What is Stripchat in simple words

Stripchat is a webcam platform where models broadcast live, and viewers watch them and chat with them in real time. In this guide for beginners, the site is disassembled from scratch: what happens here, what they pay for and how to navigate the first evening.

Stripchat differs from video sites in that everything is live. There is no recording, and there is no script either. The model reacts to viewers’ messages, answers questions, arranges games and sets goals, so yesterday’s and today’s broadcasts of the same model can be very different.

There are three types of viewers: guests without an account, registered users without purchases, and users with tokens. Their capabilities vary, but public broadcasts are open to everyone. What is available without payment is described in detail in the guide about free viewing.

First fifteen minutes on the site

You don’t have to rush with registration, and even more so with replenishing your balance. First, just look around:

  1. Open the list of broadcasts and scroll through it. The thumbnails are updated, and they show what is currently happening on the air.
  2. Go to two or three different broadcasts: a calm one, where the model chats with the audience, and a lively one, where the chat just flashes by.
  3. Understand how the room is organized: video window, chat, model information, as well as goals and a tip menu, if any.
  4. Monitor messages about tips in chat. They show how viewers interact with the model and what she offers.
  5. You will need your account when you want to write to the chat or save the model.

Such an acquaintance costs nothing, and half of the questions after it disappear on their own.

How to find a model to suit your taste

There are so many broadcasts going on simultaneously that there is no point in scrolling through them in a row. It is more convenient to use filters and categories: by appearance, age (18+ only), language and broadcast format. For Russian-speaking viewers, it is usually easier to start with models who speak Russian, then there is no need for a translator in the chat.

A couple of practical tips:

  • In addition to the thumbnail, read the profile description. Models write there what they like, what languages they speak and when they usually go on air.
  • You shouldn’t judge the quality by the number of viewers. In a small room, the model has time to respond to everyone, but in a popular room, your message can easily get lost.
  • New models are often especially willing to communicate, because they are just building an audience.
  • If you like the model, add it to your favorites (you need an account for this), and the site will tell you when it will go on air again.

Chat and communication with the model

Without chat, webcam does not work. The model reads the messages and responds by voice or text. To get noticed, simple things are enough: say hello, ask something on the topic, respond to what is happening on the air. A short “hi, where are you from?” works better than requirements and commands.

Each room has its own rules. Some models close the chat for guests or users without tokens, others ask not to capslock or spam. Before your first message, look at the room description. General standards of behavior are collected in the guide to webcam chat etiquette.

What you have to pay for if you want

Public broadcasts are free. You only have to pay if you want more: send the model a tip, choose an item from her menu, go to a private show, or buy a ticket to a special show. All this is paid for in internal currency, tokens, which are purchased in advance.

It is useful for a beginner to decide in advance how much he is willing to spend per evening, and not to top up his balance beyond this amount. Tokens are used up quickly, especially when the chat is captivating and everyone around is sending tips for a common goal.

Frequent mistakes in the first week

  • Buy a large package of tokens at once. While it is not clear what you like, a large purchase almost always ends in unnecessary expenses. Start small or don't make any purchases at all.
  • Write command models. “Show” or “do” without a greeting most quickly leads to a ban in the room. The model is a living person, and you shouldn’t treat her like a menu.
  • Wait for a response to each message. In a busy room, the model physically does not have time to read everything, and if you are not noticed, the chat is simply too fast.
  • Believe “personal suggestions.” Cheap tokens, free private conversations or messaging in messenger for a fee usually turn out to be a scam.
  • Sit in the same room. Try different broadcasts, languages and formats: each model has its own style, and it’s not the first time you find your favorite ones.

Security and privacy from day one

  • Come up with a nickname that is not associated with your name, work or other accounts.
  • Do not give models and other spectators your phone number, address, place of work, or photos of documents.
  • Do not transfer money “directly”, bypassing the platform, even if asked to do so. Payments have support on Stripchat, but outside of it there will be no one to help.
  • Register only on the official page and create a password that is not used anywhere else.

Frequently asked questions

What to do on your first visit to Stripchat?

Watch several different broadcasts without registering or purchasing anything. It will become clear what formats and models you are interested in, and then you can decide whether an account is needed.

Does the model see that I am watching her broadcast?

Usually the model sees the number of viewers and nicknames of registered users in the chat. A guest without an account is just a number on the counter for her.

Is it obligatory to tip?

Tips are sent at will, as a thank you. You can watch the public broadcast without spending a single token.

Why is the chat closed for me for some models?

The model can limit the chat, for example, leaving the right to write only to those who have tokens on their balance. This is how she set up the room, the website has nothing to do with it.
