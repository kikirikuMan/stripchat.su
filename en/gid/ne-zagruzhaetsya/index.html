Broadcasts are not loading: what to check step by step

First determine what exactly is not working

If Stripchat won't load, it's most often the browser or connection that's to blame, and you can fix it in a couple of minutes. Check everything in order and do not change several settings at once, otherwise it will not be clear what exactly helped.

Check by steps

Step 1. Update your browser

Video broadcasts work on modern web technologies, and older versions of browsers do not work well with them. Go to settings, open the “About Browser” section and install the update, if available. Then the browser needs to be restarted.

Step 2: Disable blockers for this page

Extensions against ads, trackers or scripts often mistake the built-in player for advertising and hide it. There is no need to remove the extension, just add the page to the exclusion list.

  1. Click on the extension icon next to the address bar.
  2. Select an option like “Do not block on this site” or “Pause on this page.”
  3. Reload the tab.

If your browser has built-in tracking protection, lower it for this site while checking. Settings are opened by the icon to the left of the address.

Step 3: Clear cache and cookies

To make the site open faster, the browser stores copies of its files. Over time, they become outdated, and the page begins to work crookedly. Try a hard reboot first: on a computer this is usually Ctrl+F5 or Ctrl+Shift+R, on a Mac the combination is Cmd+Shift+R. Didn't help? Clear your cache and cookies from the last few days in your privacy settings. After clearing cookies, you will have to log in to your account again.

Step 4. Check your Internet connection

  • Open another website with video. If it slows down there too, the problem is in the connection.
  • Reboot your router and wait half a minute before turning it on again.
  • Sit closer to the router or connect with a cable.
  • Pause background downloads, system updates, and other videos.

Step 5. Reduce broadcast quality

For high-definition broadcasts you need a fast and stable channel. The player usually has a gear or quality menu, where you can select a lower resolution. The picture will become a little softer, but the video will stop freezing. Broadcasts in HD It’s more convenient to watch via a stable home Internet.

Step 6: Try a different browser or device

If all else fails, open the page in another browser or on another device. So it quickly becomes clear where to look for the reason: if everything works in another browser, the problem is in the settings or extensions of the first one. You can also check in incognito mode, where extensions are usually disabled by default.

Phone features

Smartphones have their own reasons why broadcasts do not load:

  • Saving traffic in your browser or phone settings may compress or block the video. Turn it off for this site.
  • Energy saving limits background work and sometimes interferes with playback. You can turn it off while watching.
  • If the mobile signal is weak, switch to Wi-Fi or lower the video quality.
  • When memory is full, close unnecessary tabs and applications and restart the browser.

More tips on watching from a smartphone can be found on the page broadcasts from a phone.

There is video, but no sound

They also often complain about this: the picture works, but there is no sound. Usually nothing is broken, it's just a matter of settings:

  • Browsers play videos without sound until you click something on the page, so click on the speaker icon in the player;
  • look to see if the sound of the tab is muted (on a computer you can see a speaker icon on it, you can turn the sound on by right-clicking);
  • check that the system's sound is turned on and the desired output device is selected, such as headphones;
  • on your phone, look at the side switch for silent mode and media volume.

Some models broadcast with music or without sound at all, this is what they decided for themselves. If there is no sound in only one room, look in another.

If the problem persists

Sometimes a failure occurs on the side of the platform itself, and then you just have to wait and try later. If you have an account and the problem persists, write to the site's support service. Please indicate your device, browser and version, and describe what is happening.

Do not download “special players” and “accelerators” from third-party sites. A regular updated browser is enough for broadcasting, but such programs often turn out to be malicious. How to protect yourself is described in the guide about anonymity and security.

If you have gone through the entire list, try opening the broadcasts again.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there a black screen instead of broadcasting?

Most often, the video is hidden by a blocker extension or interfered with by an outdated browser. Disable the blocker for the page, refresh your browser, and reload the tab.

Why does the video keep stopping and buffering?

As a rule, the Internet speed is not enough. Select a lower quality in the player, stop background downloads and check the connection.

It works on the computer, but not on the phone. What to do?

Update your mobile browser, turn off data saving and try a different browser. If this doesn't help, restart your phone.

Only one model does not load. Is this an error?

Most likely, everything is fine. The model could end the broadcast, go private, or encounter communication problems on her side. Open another broadcast.
