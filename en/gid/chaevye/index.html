Tipping in webcam chat: how and why to thank a model

What is a tip on Stripchat

Tips on Stripchat are tokens that the viewer sends to the model directly during the broadcast. Public broadcasting remains free, and you pay for a specific action, for a request, or simply as a thank you for a good mood.

The model earns money in the general chat mainly from tips, and with their help the viewer can influence what happens on the air. Everyone can see the submission: a line with your nickname and amount appears in the chat, and the model usually responds personally. If it is not yet clear what kind of internal currency this is and how to top it up, first look at the guide about tokens.

Tip menu: price list directly on the screen

Almost every model has a tip menu, that is, a list of actions with prices in tokens. It is displayed next to the video or attached to the room description. The items can be very different: say your name, dance to the selected song, change clothes, turn on a toy for a few seconds or answer a question.

A few tips to avoid awkwardness with the menu:

  • Read carefully. Items with the same name in different models have different meanings and mean different things, and each model performs them according to its own rules.
  • Send exactly the amount indicated. Many models set up an automatic response to a certain number of tokens, and if you send more tokens, another item will work or nothing at all.
  • If something is not on the menu, you can ask politely, but you cannot demand it. The model has the right to refuse and not explain anything.
  • Wait a little. When someone else has already sent a tip before you, your item will be fulfilled next.

Goals: a common goal for the whole room

A goal is a token target that a model sets during a stream. Usually this is a certain amount of tokens, after which the promised show will begin. Progress is visible on the bar above the video or in the description line, and any viewer can contribute their part.

The convenience of a goal is that you don’t have to pay the entire amount to one person: five people little by little give the same amount as one generous guest. A couple of details worth knowing:

  • Sometimes goals come one after another, and the description of the next one appears only when the current one is closed.
  • If the goal is still far away, and the broadcast will end soon, the show may be postponed. When it's important to you, ask in chat.
  • Tokens sent to a goal will not be returned, even if the goal is not reached.

How much to give and when

There is no correct amount. One viewer will send a couple of tokens in response to a smile, another will save up for a big goal. Follow your budget and don’t follow others, because there will always be someone in the chat who spends more.

When tipping is especially appropriate:

  • the model fulfilled your request from the tip menu or answered the question in detail;
  • you sat in the room for a long time, and you felt good there;
  • the broadcast has just started, there are few people yet, and your support is easy to notice;
  • the goal is missing quite a bit, and your contribution will fill it.

When you want one-on-one attention, it can be more profitable to immediately go to a private show than to send a tip a little at a time. There the payment is per minute, and all this time the model is engaged only with you.

How not to overspend

Live broadcast is captivating, and you can send tokens in a second. To prevent the evening from ending in an unpleasant surprise, keep a few rules in mind:

  1. Determine in advance how much you are willing to spend per session, and buy a package of tokens for this amount. It’s not worth purchasing in small portions during the broadcast.
  2. If a model or other viewers pressure you to tip, you do not owe them anything.
  3. Check the amount before sending. It's especially easy to miss a button on a phone.
  4. Free viewing is also a normal option: you can watch and write in chat without spending a single token.

How to safely top up your balance is described in the guide about purchasing tokens.

Common mistakes of beginners

  • Tip in advance for promises. If there is no item in the menu and your request is answered with “tokens first”, find out the conditions before sending a large amount.
  • Dispatching when the model has moved away. The tokens will arrive, but you will have to wait for a response, so first make sure that the model is in the frame and looking into the chat.
  • Confusion between goal and menu. The amount “just like that” usually goes to the current goal and the menu item does not launch. Check the room description.
  • Expectation of special treatment. They thank you with tips; they can’t buy friendship. A model can highlight a generous guest, but she has no obligations.

Tipping and good manners

Sent tokens do not give the right to be rude. The model is the hostess of her room: she sets the rules, can refuse a request and block an intrusive guest, no matter how many he sends. A polite message along with tokens always works better than requirements.

Do not transfer money to models or “their managers” outside the site. Tips are sent only through the balance of tokens on the platform: then you have a history of transactions and there is support to contact. More information about protecting your data is written in the guide about anonymity.

Select a room, open the tip menu and watch how the model responds to tips from other guests. It will become clear pretty quickly what you like.

Frequently asked questions

Is it mandatory to tip on Stripchat?

No, you can watch public broadcasts for free and do not send anything. Tips are used to thank the model or trigger an action from her menu, and only if desired.

Is it possible to return tips sent?

As a rule, this is not possible: tokens are credited to the model immediately. Therefore, before sending, check the amount and the selected menu item.

Can other viewers see how much I sent?

Tips usually appear in the general chat along with the sender's nickname. If you don't want to attract attention, take a neutral nickname and look at your account privacy settings.

What happens if the goal is not collected before the end of the broadcast?

The model itself decides: some move the goal to the next broadcast, others start a new one. Tokens sent to the goal remain with the model in any case.
