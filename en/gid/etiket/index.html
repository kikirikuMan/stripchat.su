Webcam chat etiquette: rules for communicating with models

Why do we need etiquette

Webcam chat etiquette is not just for show. The model's room is both her workplace and a small community of spectators. When the chat is calm and friendly, the model feels freer, the show is livelier, and you get more attention, even if you haven't spent a single token.

The rules of communication on Stripchat are much the same as common courtesy in any online community. There are also some peculiarities, and it is useful to know them in advance, especially if you are just starting.

First minutes in the room

  1. Read the room description and pinned messages. Usually the model writes its rules, tip menu and current goal there.
  2. Just observe for a couple of minutes: what language they speak, what the mood is, who the moderators are.
  3. Say hello briefly, addressing the model by name. Enough with the usual “hello, how are you?”
  4. Didn't answer right away? Don't repeat the greeting ten times, because she can read dozens of messages at the same time.

What models value

  • Live conversation

    Ask about the music in the background, about a hobby, or about how your day went. Models hear the same type of requests all the time, and they are happy to have a normal interlocutor.

  • Compliments without vulgarity

    Note a smile, outfit, interior or sense of humor. A specific compliment will be remembered better than a general one.

  • Patience

    If a model is performing a menu item for another viewer or responding to someone in a private show, wait your turn.

  • Gratitude

    Say “thank you” after an answer or show. You can add tip, but even without them, gratitude will be in place.

What is considered rude

For some things, a model or moderator will block you without hesitation, even if you were just joking:

  • demands instead of requests: “show”, “do”, “come quickly” without context and without tokens are read as an order;
  • spam, that is, the same message many times, a continuous capslock or a stream of emoticons;
  • negative remarks about the figure, age, skin or voice (if you don’t like the model, it’s easier to move to another room);
  • questions about personal information: real name, city, address, social networks, offers to meet;
  • bargaining over prices in the tip menu or for private shows, because the prices are set by the model;
  • comparisons like “but that model is cheaper”, which do not inspire anyone;
  • Advertise your channels and links to third-party sites in someone else’s room.

The boundaries of the model are the law of the room

Each model has its own list of what it does and doesn't do. It is written in the profile, in the tip menu or spoken on air. If the model said no, the topic is closed. Don’t persuade, don’t offer to “double the amount” and don’t start the same conversation ten minutes later.

The same rule applies in private shows. By paying for private show, you get the model’s time and attention, and her rules continue to apply. Anything that is not included in its format is not worth discussing.

The model has the right to end the show, kick out the viewer or block him without explaining anything. This is part of her job, and there is nothing to be offended about.

Sometimes a rude message differs from a pleasant one in just a couple of words. Here are some examples:

Communicate with other viewers

Dozens of people are sitting in a popular room at the same time, and they are also worth taking into account. Don't argue in the general chat, don't laugh at other people's messages, and don't brag about your tips. If someone behaves ugly, do not get into a fight, the moderators and the model will deal with him. As a last resort, you can report the user or add him to ignore.

Etiquette in a private show

In a private show, communication becomes personal, but the basic rules do not go away. Here's what to remember:

  • Start with a couple of phrases about your mood. This makes it easier for both to relax and tune in to each other.
  • Say your wishes in advance. It’s more convenient for a model when it can immediately confirm that this is included in its format.
  • Keep an eye on the time. Privat is paid per minute, and you will have to calculate your budget yourself; the model is not obliged to remind you of this.
  • Don't record the show. Recording and distributing broadcasts without the consent of the model violates the rules of the platforms and her right to privacy.
  • Say goodbye. A simple “thank you, it was a pleasure” leaves a good impression, and next time you will be greeted as an acquaintance.

Language of communication

Models from different countries usually understand English, and many also understand Russian. In a foreign language, write in simple short phrases, they are easier to read and translate. If you want to communicate in your native language without any barriers, take a look at the Russian models section.

You can use an online translator, just re-read the result: machine translation sometimes turns a harmless phrase into an ambiguous one. If the model does not understand you, reformulate it more simply, and do not repeat it with a capslock. And don't be offended if she answers in her own language. Many models read better than they write.

Walk into the room, say hello and ask the model something. One polite message is enough for a good acquaintance.

Frequently asked questions

Can I write models if I don't send tips?

Of course. It is quite normal to communicate politely without tokens, and many models are happy to have interlocutors. Just don't demand anything and don't clog up the chat.

Why was I blocked in the room?

The model or moderator of the room is blocked for rudeness, spam, intrusive demands or violation of the rules from the description. The model always has the last word.

Who are the moderators in the model room?

Trusted viewers whom the model asked to keep order in the chat. They can remove messages and restrict violators.

Is it possible to ask a model to meet in real life?

Not worth it. Such requests are perceived as rude and violate the rules of most models, so communication remains on the platform.
