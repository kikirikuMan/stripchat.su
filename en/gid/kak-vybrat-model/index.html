How to choose a webcam model that you like

Where to start choosing

Choosing a model on Stripchat can be difficult precisely because of the abundance. There are a lot of online broadcasts at any time, and half an hour is easily spent scrolling through the previews. Here are ways to narrow your search and quickly find the broadcast you like.

Before opening filters, it is useful to understand a couple of things for yourself. Are you more interested in a conversation or a show? What language do you want to communicate in? Are you ready to spend tokens, or will viewing be enough for now? The answers determine what to look at first.

Filters and categories

Filters cut off the excess quickly. Models can be selected by appearance, age group (all models are strictly adults), country, language and broadcast format. The set of filters changes from time to time, but the principle is the same: the more precise the request, the shorter the list.

If you don’t want to bother with filters, start with ready-made selections in the category catalog. For example:

  • new models, who are often especially attentive to the audience;
  • models with interactive toys, if you like to influence the show with tips;
  • couples, themed shows, cosplay or dances when a specific format is needed.

Language matters

Many viewers come to webcams for the sake of communication, and then language becomes decisive. A model who understands you without a translator, jokes back and keeps the conversation going is usually more interesting than the most impressive but silent model.

Language can be found:

  • in the model’s profile, in the field with the languages spoken;
  • in the name or description of the room;
  • in the chat, based on how viewers write and how the model responds.

At the same time, pay attention to how the model communicates: by voice or only by text. Some are willing to speak out loud and answer questions, others play music and write briefly in the chat. If a live voice is important to you, this will become clear within a couple of minutes.

When a profile lists multiple languages, the native one is usually listed first. Almost everyone understands English, but for fluent conversation it is better to look for a match in your native language.

What to look for in the room

The preview shows only the picture. To understand whether this is your model, open the air and sit there for a few minutes. It is convenient to evaluate based on the following criteria:

  • Picture and sound quality

    Good light, stable video and clear sound usually mean that the model takes its work seriously.

  • Reaction to chat

    See if she responds to viewers, addresses them by name and carries on a conversation, or just looks at the camera.

  • Chat environment

    It’s more pleasant to sit in a calm, friendly chat with moderators than in the middle of spam and demands.

  • Tip menu and goal

    The menu shows what format the model offers and how much it costs. If nothing from there interests you, look further.

  • Profile description

    Many models write there about themselves and their interests, about the rules of the room and schedule.

Popularity, ratings and favorites

The number of spectators in the room indicates popularity, but it may not suit your taste. When there are a thousand people in the room, the model physically does not have time to answer everyone, and in a small broadcast there are much more chances for a normal conversation.

More useful is your own rating, that is, a list of favorites. Add everyone you like even a little there, and in a week you will see who you return to more often. Favorites open after free registration, and then the site will be able to show which of your models is currently on air.

The time of day also affects the choice

Models live in different time zones, and different people are on air throughout the day. Some are online in the morning and afternoon in Moscow, others are online late in the evening. If your favorite model goes on air while you are at work, it makes sense to look for a similar one whose schedule coincides with your free time.

A couple of practical tips:

  • visit the same category at different times to get an idea of who is usually online during your hours;
  • look at the profile, some models indicate the days and times of broadcasts there;
  • please note that during peak hours the rooms are crowded, and during quiet times the model can pay more attention to each viewer.

Look before you pay

Do not waste tokens on a model you see for the first time. Public broadcasts on Stripchat are free, so you can take a closer look first.

  1. Watch the public broadcast for at least ten to fifteen minutes.
  2. Write a couple of messages in the chat and see how the model responds.
  3. If you liked it, send a small tip and watch the reaction.
  4. After that, it makes sense to consider a private show or a larger goal.
You liked the model, but there’s no time for it now? Add it to your favorites. There are good broadcasts every day, there is no rush.

Look for yours

If “the same” model was not found the first time, it’s okay. Sometimes it takes a couple of evenings to understand what is closer to you: calm conversational broadcasts, fun rooms with goals or interactive shows. Save everyone you are interested in as favorites, and then remove those you don’t return to.

Tastes change over time. Sometimes look into unfamiliar categories; there are often unexpected favorites there. If you come across unclear terms in your profiles, look them up in webcam dictionary.

Frequently asked questions

How to find a model who speaks Russian?

Turn on a filter by language or open the Russian models section. In addition, the language of communication is usually indicated in the model's profile.

How not to lose the model you like?

Add it to your favorites. You will need a free account, but then the site will show you when it will go on air again.

Is it worth going private with a new model right away?

First, it’s better to watch the public broadcast and chat. This way, before payment, it will become clear whether its style and format are suitable for you.

Why hasn’t the favorite model appeared for a long time?

The models choose their own schedule. Some work according to a schedule, others go on air when they can or take breaks. Sometimes the schedule is in the profile.
