Stripchat tokens: the platform's internal currency

What are tokens

Tokens on Stripchat are the internal currency with which viewers pay for everything paid on the platform: tips, private shows, tickets to special broadcasts. You buy a package for regular money, tokens are credited to your account balance, and with each action the required amount is debited from there.

The model also indicates its prices in tokens, for example, per minute of a private show or per menu item. This is more convenient for the viewer: card details do not need to be entered each time, and all expenses are calculated in one currency.

Why currency if you can pay with money

  • Speed. Tips can be left with one click right during the broadcast. If every payment went through the bank, a live conversation would be interrupted every now and then while waiting for confirmation.
  • Unified prices. Viewers from different countries see the same numbers in tokens, and the model does not have to think about exchange rates.
  • Privacy. In the bank statement, only the purchase of the package is visible; individual actions on the site do not appear there.
  • Control. The balance works as a limit: until you replenish it yourself, you will not be able to spend more than is in the account.

What are tokens spent on

  • Tipping

    The most common option. You send the model as many tokens as you want, and a prominent message appears in the chat. This is how they thank, ask for attention, or invest in a common goal. Details and unwritten rules are collected in the guide about tipping.

  • Tip menu

    The model itself creates a list of actions with prices in tokens: song, dance, answer to a question, change of image. You select an item, send the required amount, and the model carries it out.

  • Air goals

    The model announces a common goal, and the audience gathers it together. You can invest as much as you want, the whole room will see the result.

  • Interactive toys

    Some models connect devices to the broadcast that respond to tips: the amount is larger and the reaction is stronger or longer. Such broadcasts are collected in the category with interactive toys.

  • Private formats

    Private, exclusive private shows, voyeurism and group shows are paid per minute. How they differ from each other is discussed in the guide about types of shows.

  • Ticket shows

    The model announces a special show and sells tickets for it. You pay once for entry; there are no per-minute fees.

  • Additional features

    Depending on the model and platform, personal messages, subscription to the model’s fan club and other options may be available for tokens. Conditions are shown when you select a function.

How much do tokens and actions cost

Prices here are arranged in two layers. First comes the cost of the tokens themselves in money. It depends on the size of the package (for large packages one token is usually cheaper), on the region and on current offers. Then come the model’s prices in tokens, and each model sets them itself: one has cheaper prices for private shows, the other has more expensive menu items. Therefore, for the same amount you can get very different things.

There are no specific numbers in the guide. They change, and any table quickly becomes outdated. Before each paid action, the platform shows how many tokens will be written off, and before purchasing a package, writes its price in your currency.

Good habit: at least approximately convert model prices into money. If you remember how much the package cost, it’s easy to calculate the price of one token, and at the same time a minute of a private show or a menu item. The numbers in tokens look “toy-like”, which is why they are underestimated. When you convert it into a familiar currency, it’s easier to soberly decide what you really don’t mind spending on.

Without which tokens are not needed

You don't need tokens to watch public broadcasts, and many viewers don't buy them at all. True, sometimes the capabilities in the chat depend on the balance. Some models, in order to cut off spam, only allow those who have tokens to write. This is a room-specific setting.

Mistakes that cause token loss

  • Blind tipping. The person sends the amount without opening the model’s menu, and then is surprised that nothing happened. If you are waiting for a specific action, find it in the menu and send exactly the amount indicated there.
  • An extra zero. When you enter the amount manually, it is easy to make a mistake. Before you send, look at the number again, especially on your phone.
  • A private show without prior agreement. Minutes count from the first second, and if half the private show time is spent figuring out what you want, the balance is wasted. Discuss everything in the chat in advance.
  • Forgotten show. In per-minute formats, tokens are debited as long as the show is running. If you move away from the screen, end it first.

How not to spend too much

  1. Determine the amount you are willing to spend per month and buy tokens only within its limits.
  2. Before going private, check the price of a minute and calculate how many minutes you can afford.
  3. Don't get worked up over a common goal. The phrase “just a little bit left” is constantly heard in chats.
  4. If the platform shows your spending history, look at it occasionally. You can immediately see where the balance actually goes.

Frequently asked questions

How many rubles does one token cost?

There is no single course. The price depends on the package size, region and current offers, and you will see the exact cost on the purchase page before payment.

Do purchased tokens expire?

This is determined by the platform rules. As a rule, tokens remain on your account while you use it, but it is better to check the latest conditions in the Stripchat rules.

Is it possible to return tokens back to money?

Viewer tokens are needed to pay for opportunities on the platform, and they are usually not exchanged back into money. If the write-off seems erroneous, write to support.

Can tokens be transferred to another user?

Tokens are spent on models and shows within the platform; there is no usual transfer function between viewers. So feel free to ignore offers to “sell tokens.”
