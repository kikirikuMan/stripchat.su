Webcam chat from the inside: messages, private messages, moderation

What is webcam chat

Webcam chat is text correspondence next to a live broadcast of the model. The video goes one way, from the model to the audience, and the communication is two-way: you write, the model reads and responds by voice or in chat. Therefore, video chat on a webcam site is more like a conversation in a small company than watching a video.

Below we will look at what such an online chat consists of, who keeps order and what functions it has. There will be a little talk about manners here; there is a separate etiquette guide for this purpose.

What does the chat window consist of

The interfaces of the platforms differ, but the main parts are almost the same everywhere:

  • General feed, where messages from all viewers of the room with nicknames and status icons appear in order.
  • Input field. You can type text in it, add emoticons and send tips.
  • System messages about tips, the start of the show, goal progress and model status changes.
  • List of viewers with nicknames of registered users. Guests are usually visible only in the counter.
  • Description of the room where the model keeps the rules, menu and schedule.

As a rule, only users with an account write to the general feed. The guest can watch the broadcast and read the correspondence, but the input field is closed for him.

General chat and private messages

In the general feed, the conversation is general: the model responds to the person she noticed, the rest read. In a live room, messages travel quickly, and if yours gets lost, it's okay.

Private messages go through a separate channel between you and the model; they are not visible to other viewers. A model’s personal account is often limited, say, open only to those who have bought something or have a certain status. This is how they avoid spam. An inactive submit button means exactly this model setting, the site has nothing to do with it.

A personal message is not a reason to take communication away from the platform. The request to go to the messenger or pay “directly” is one of the favorite tricks of scammers.

Smileys, tips and nickname color

You can stand out in a webcam chat not only with text. Smileys and stickers allow you to react without interrupting the conversation. Tips appear in the feed as a prominent line with a nickname and amount, and the model usually thanks them out loud. When a tip is tied to a menu item, you can see in the chat what exactly was ordered.

On many platforms, a user's status is shown by a color or icon next to their nickname. Using it, the model understands who has entered the room for the first time and who has been visiting the room for a long time. How the menu and goals work is described in detail in the guide about tipping.

Settings that make chat more convenient

When there are many people in the room, the tape turns into a continuous stream. Almost all platforms have settings to customize it for yourself:

  • Tipping notifications. If the token lines make it difficult to follow the conversation, they can usually be minimized or disabled.
  • Ignore. The obsessive viewer's posts will disappear from your feed, although others will still be able to see them.
  • Sound. Alarms for new messages and tips are muted separately from the broadcast sound.
  • Phone. In a mobile browser, the chat usually either lies on top of the video or opens in a separate tab. Try both vertical and horizontal screen positions.

Where exactly these points are located and what they are called depends on the site. Look in the chat menu or account settings.

Who keeps order

  • Model

    She is the owner of the room and can delete a message, temporarily prohibit writing or block the viewer.

  • Room moderators

    Trusted viewers. The model instructs them to monitor the feed while she broadcasts.

  • Platform filters

    They automatically hide links, contacts, advertisements and banned words before anyone sees them.

  • Site support

    Complaints about users and models go here when the problem is wider than one room.

Cum-cam: when the model sees you

In cam-to-cam mode (cam2cam), the viewer also turns on the camera, and the model sees him. Usually this mode is available in a private show and only if both parties agree. Before turning on the camera, look at what is in the frame: a face, documents on the table, the view from the window. Other viewers cannot see you. More details about the formats of personal shows are written in the guide about private shows.

Language and translation

In a webcam chat with girls from different countries, the first thing that comes up is the question of language. There are two ways to solve this. You can immediately search for models who speak Russian; for this there is a section Russian models and a filter by language. Or you can use translation: some platforms translate messages directly in the chat, and many models understand simple English. Write in short phrases and without slang, then the meaning will not be lost even in machine translation.

What not to do in adult chat

The adult format does not mean that everything is allowed. The rules of the platforms and the models themselves usually prohibit:

  • publish links, contacts and advertisements;
  • share your own or others’ personal data;
  • insult the model and other viewers, spam and write with capslock;
  • offer meetings and payment outside the site;
  • insist on what the model has already refused.

Violators have messages deleted and blocked in the room, and in serious cases, the entire account is limited.

Frequently asked questions

Do other viewers see my personal messages from the model?

They don’t see. The personal message is available only to you and the model; it does not appear in the general feed of the room.

Why didn’t my message appear in the chat?

Most likely, it was hidden by an automatic filter, for example due to a link, phone number or a prohibited word. It also happens that the model has closed the chat for guests or for users without tokens.

Is it possible to correspond with a model if she is not on air?

Sometimes you can: on some platforms, models read private messages and respond later. It all depends on the model settings and site rules.

Is it necessary to turn on the camera in webcam chat?

Not at all necessary. The viewer writes in text and remains invisible, and his own camera is needed only for cam-to-cam. It's up to you to decide whether to turn it on or not.
