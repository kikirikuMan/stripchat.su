Stripchat show types: from public streams to private shows

Why are there so many formats

A private show on Stripchat is just one of several show formats. There are also public broadcasts, exclusive private shows, voyeurism mode, group and ticket shows. They differ in the composition of participants, who controls what is happening, and how tokens are written off. When you understand the difference, it’s easier to choose a format that suits your mood and budget.

Each model sets its own prices, so there will be no numbers in this guide. The cost of any format is visible in the room even before payment.

Public broadcast

It all begins with him. The broadcast is visible to everyone who logs in, and you can watch it for free. The model communicates with the chat, answers questions, completes items from the tip menu and collects common goals. Usually this is where you get to know it and decide whether you want to continue in a different format.

Private show

You send the model a request for a private show. If she accepts it, the public broadcast is closed for others. You pay by the minute: while the show is on, every minute the price assigned by the model is removed from the balance. It turns out to be a one-on-one conversation, and you can discuss what interests you, within the rules of the platform and the boundaries that the model said.

Keep in mind one thing: other viewers can enter a regular private chat in voyeur mode. They can't write or direct the show, but they watch. If this bothers you, take the exclusive.

Exclusive private show

The same private show, only without observers: you see the show alone. It usually costs more per minute because the model loses money on voyeurs. They take an exclusive when complete privacy and the full attention of the model are important, or when you want to talk calmly without the feeling that someone is standing behind the glass.

Peeping

This is an ordinary private conversation, seen from the outside. If the model is currently in a private show with another viewer, the observer can connect to him. As a rule, it costs less than your own privacy, but you won’t decide anything: you can’t write models and you can’t influence the course of the show either. The mode is useful if you first want to see how the model conducts private conversations, and then order your own.

Group show

The model announces a group show, the audience joins him. The required number of participants has been collected, and the show starts, and everyone pays for their place. It turns out cheaper than an individual private show, because the cost is actually divided among the whole group. There are also disadvantages: if there aren’t enough people, the show may never start, and the model’s attention gets to everyone at once.

Ticket show

The model announces such a show in advance and indicates the topic, approximate time and ticket price. You pay one time for admission and watch the entire show, without paying per minute. It’s convenient for the budget because you know the amount from the very beginning. Follow the announcement and start time, otherwise part of the show will take place without you.

What to expect from a private chat and what not to do

The main thing that a private show gives is the attention of the model. On public broadcast it is divided into tens and hundreds of viewers, but here it goes to you. You can calmly talk, ask for what interests you, and see the reaction to yourself personally. Many viewers go private primarily for the sake of conversation.

A private show still has boundaries. The model herself determines what she is ready to do, she can refuse a request or close the show if she is rude. The rules of the platform in private mode do not go away, and attempts to arrange a meeting, correspondence in instant messengers, or pay “over the counter” quickly end in blocking. And one more thing: a private show does not promise a “special relationship”. The model is friendly because this is her job. If you keep this in mind, you will save both your nerves and your wallet.

It happens that in the public air the model seems hasty or absent-minded, but in a private show she reveals itself completely differently. If you like the lively, noisy room itself, it may be better to stay on the public air and support the tip model.

How to choose a format and prepare

  1. First, watch the model on public air. A private chat with a person you have never seen is like a lottery, and a couple of minutes in a general chat will tell you a lot about the model. We discussed how to choose it in the guide about choosing a model.
  2. Check the price of a minute and estimate how many minutes your balance will last.
  3. If you have a modest budget, you should take a closer look at group and ticketed shows, where the final amount is clearer.
  4. Before the private conversation, write briefly in the chat what you want and ask if the model agrees. This way you will save minutes and avoid awkward pauses.

The rules of politeness in any format are the same as in the general chat. Respect the boundaries of the model, do not put pressure on her and do not try to take communication outside the platform. More information about this is written in the guide to etiket.

Frequently asked questions

How does a private differ from an exclusive private?

Viewers can look into a regular private in voyeur mode, but the exclusive is designed only for the two of you. That's why it usually costs more.

Does the model see those who spy on private?

The peeping tom simply watches someone else's private message and does not affect the show. How the model sees these viewers depends on the platform interface.

What happens if the tokens run out during a private session?

The minute-by-minute show will end as soon as there is not enough balance for the next minute. If you want to chat longer, top up your account in advance.

Can a model refuse privacy?

Maybe. She decides whether to accept the request, and on some days she turns off private shows altogether. There is no need to be offended by a refusal: it is her right.
