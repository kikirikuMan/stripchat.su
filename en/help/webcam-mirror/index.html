How to mirror a webcam preview and check the result

A mirrored preview swaps left and right. It may make self-view feel more familiar, but it can also reverse writing. Before changing anything, establish whether you want to change your own preview or the image another person receives.

On this page

  1. Use an asymmetric reference
  2. Compare both views in the local test
  3. Check what the other participant sees
  4. Avoid correcting the image twice

Use an asymmetric reference

Hold up a sheet with an ordinary word or place an object on one side of the scene. A face alone makes left-right orientation harder to assess. Look at the lettering and object position rather than relying on how familiar your reflection feels.

A horizontal flip is different from rotating an upside-down image. If the camera is inverted, first check its mounting and the app's rotation controls.

Compare both views in the local test

This control only flips the displayed preview on this page. It does not change your camera driver, configure another application, create a recording or send video to anyone. Opening a meeting app requires a separate check of that app's behavior.

  1. Open the webcam test, start the camera and grant permission if requested.
  2. Change Mirror this preview and compare the same word or object in both positions.
  3. Leave the checkbox in the position you prefer, then stop the test when finished.

Check what the other participant sees

Ask a participant to read the word you are holding up. Compare their report with your self-view before adding a second flip. A reversed self-view can coexist with readable outgoing video.

For example, Microsoft documents Mirror my video in Teams Free on desktop as a change to your own view. Do not assume a similarly named setting in every other app has the same scope; consult its documentation and test it.

Avoid correcting the image twice

If camera software, a virtual camera and the calling app all offer transformations, record which controls you changed. Two horizontal flips can cancel each other, leaving a confusing chain of settings.

Restore the last change when the result becomes less clear. For a demonstration with writing or labeled objects, prioritize a readable outgoing picture and verify it in the final app. A preference for mirrored self-view should not decide that result by itself.
