Test your webcam before a call

A camera test is useful when it answers a specific question: does the picture appear, is this the intended camera, and does it keep updating? Use the short check below before changing drivers or buying another device.

Local device test

Start only the device you want to check. The camera preview and microphone level stay in this browser; no recording is created. The test stops when this tab is hidden.

The test has not started.

  2. Read the result

Start with one camera test

Close any camera preview already open in another tab or app. Place something recognizable in view, such as your hand, so you can tell a live picture from a frozen frame.

  1. Press Start camera and respond to the browser's permission request.
  2. Move your hand across the picture, then sit where you will be during the call.
  3. If the wrong camera appears, check the browser's camera choice, stop the test and restart it.

Read the result

A moving preview confirms that this browser can receive video from the selected source right now. It does not measure the quality another person will receive, test your microphone, or certify a camera's advertised resolution.

For example, a sharp preview with a dark face points toward lighting or exposure. A picture that never appears calls for a connection or permission check first.

Separate browser and device problems

Compare the result with the computer's camera app, opening one test at a time. If that app also fails, note whether the camera is missing entirely or listed with a black picture. These are different symptoms.

If the camera app works and this page does not, use the permissions guide. Record the exact browser message before changing anything; it gives a more useful starting point than “the camera is broken.”

Finish in the app you will use

Press Stop when the preview is no longer needed. Then open the intended meeting app and check its own preview and camera selection. A successful browser test cannot confirm every setting in a separate app.

For a repeatable comparison, leave the camera position and lighting unchanged between tests. Change one setting, retry the same movement, and keep the change only if it helps.

Privacy

The test starts only when you press a start button and the browser permits access. Video and audio are processed locally in your browser; this tool does not record or upload them. The hosting service still receives normal HTTP requests when you load this page.
