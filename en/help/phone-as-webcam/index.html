Use your phone as a webcam

A phone can provide a useful camera when a laptop's built-in picture is poor. First choose a method supported by both devices: installing unrelated apps at random makes connection problems harder to isolate.

On this page

  1. Choose the route before installing anything
  2. Prepare a stable position
  3. Select the phone in the receiving app
  4. Isolate pauses and disconnects

Choose the route before installing anything

For a compatible Android phone and Windows 11 PC, Microsoft's connected-camera feature is one option. For an iPhone and Mac, check Apple's Continuity Camera requirements. Iriun is a separate app-based route covered in its own guide; its availability and requirements should be checked for your exact devices.

Write down the phone model, mobile OS and computer OS. A method working on someone else's phone does not establish compatibility with yours. Keep the manufacturer's current requirements open while setting up.

Prepare a stable position

  1. Place the phone in a secure holder with the intended lens unobstructed. Clean that lens and check the framing before joining a call.
  2. Keep cables clear of the stand and leave space around the phone for ventilation. Check battery level before a longer session.
  3. Close other camera apps on the phone. Use a trusted local connection and follow the selected provider's pairing prompts.

Select the phone in the receiving app

After pairing, choose the phone explicitly in the computer app's camera menu. A successful pairing message alone does not mean that app has switched away from the laptop camera. Cover the phone lens briefly to identify the active source.

Check audio separately. A phone video source does not guarantee that the meeting app selected the microphone you intended. Stop this site's device test before opening the receiving app's preview.

Isolate pauses and disconnects

If the picture stops, note whether the phone app stopped, the connection disappeared or only one computer app froze. Test the same route with a shorter session and unchanged lighting. Change one element at a time.

A warm phone, depleted battery or unstable stand is a reason to stop and correct the setup. If repeated interruptions persist, use a known working camera for the call and investigate afterward. Do not assume USB is supported by every app merely because it supports wireless pairing.
