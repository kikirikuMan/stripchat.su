Camera in use by another app: what to check

A camera can work in one app and refuse to open in another. Test for a conflict by leaving one camera app open at a time. An error saying the device could not be read is a clue, not proof that another app owns it.

On this page

  1. Record the symptom before closing apps
  2. Reduce the test to one camera user
  3. If the error remains with other apps closed
  4. Finish with a repeatable result

Record the symptom before closing apps

Note which app works, which fails and the exact error message. A denied permission, missing camera and failed attempt to open a listed camera need different checks. Do not change several permissions just because one app says the camera is busy.

If you are currently in a meeting, save your work and leave it before testing. Closing a camera app will interrupt its video.

Reduce the test to one camera user

A successful retry identifies a useful workaround. It does not establish that every camera or operating system prevents simultaneous access.

  1. Stop previews in other browser tabs, meeting apps and camera configuration utilities.
  2. Close those apps normally. Check the notification area or menu bar for a utility that remained running after its window closed.
  3. Reload the failing page and try the camera once. If it starts, stop the test before reopening another app.
  4. Reopen the other apps individually, repeating the same check to identify when the conflict returns.

If the error remains with other apps closed

Try the system camera app by itself, then close it and retry the browser. If only the browser fails, check its selected camera and permissions. If both fail, check the USB connection or physical camera switch before reinstalling anything.

A browser NotReadableError can also reflect an operating-system or hardware problem. Keep the message in your notes instead of treating it as the name of a confirmed cause.

Finish with a repeatable result

If the owner of the camera is still unclear, save your work, restart the computer and open only the intended app. Compare this clean start with the earlier result.

For a managed computer, send support the camera model, failing app, error and the one-app test result. Avoid disabling security controls or repeatedly terminating unfamiliar system processes.
