How to add and check a webcam in OBS Studio

Use a simple scene to verify that OBS receives the intended camera before adding a frame or more sources. A local scene preview is enough for this check; you do not need to start recording or connect a broadcasting account.

On this page

  1. Add the camera as a video source
  2. Separate a hidden source from a failed camera
  3. Place the picture without distorting it
  4. Change capture settings only after a working baseline

Add the camera as a video source

  1. Stop other camera tests and open a new or existing OBS scene intended for this check.
  2. Use the plus button in Sources and add Video Capture Device on Windows or macOS. On Linux, the corresponding source is Video Capture Device (V4L2).
  3. Give the source a recognizable name, then select the physical camera in its device properties.
  4. Begin with the device's default settings and confirm that the preview changes when you move.

Separate a hidden source from a failed camera

If the camera appears in its properties but not on the scene canvas, check the source's visibility and position. Another source above it can cover the picture. Temporarily hide the covering source to test that explanation.

If the device properties also show no picture, verify the selected camera, operating-system permission and other apps using it. A layout adjustment cannot repair a device OBS has not opened.

Place the picture without distorting it

Select the camera source and move or resize its bounding box in the preview. Use Transform and Edit Transform when you need precise values. Keep the proportions natural instead of stretching a face to fill a differently shaped area.

If the picture needs to fit a narrower opening, decide deliberately what may be cropped. Check the head and hands during normal movement, not just while sitting still. Leave enough space for the purpose of the shot.

Change capture settings only after a working baseline

When the default picture works, note its settings before trying another supported resolution or frame rate. Unsupported combinations can produce no picture. Restore the working choice if a change fails.

Confirm that the scene remains correct after selecting another scene and returning. Check audio separately: a visible webcam image is not a microphone test. When finished, close or deactivate the camera source so the next application can use it.

Privacy

The test starts only when you press a start button and the browser permits access. Video and audio are processed locally in your browser; this tool does not record or upload them. The hosting service still receives normal HTTP requests when you load this page.
