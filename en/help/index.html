Camera and microphone help

Check your devices before a call and follow the guide that matches the problem you can see. The optional tests run in your browser after you start them and grant access.

    Use a repeatable lighting check for your webcam: face the light, reduce backlighting and reflections, then compare before changing exposure settings.

Privacy

The test starts only when you press a start button and the browser permits access. Video and audio are processed locally in your browser; this tool does not record or upload them. The hosting service still receives normal HTTP requests when you load this page.
