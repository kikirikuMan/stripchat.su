Safety and privacy for webcam models

Why think about security in advance

Webcam model security should be considered before the first live broadcast. It is much easier to prevent leakage than to delete the record later or try to return anonymity. Most of the measures are simple and do not require any technical knowledge.

Below are the rules used by the experimental models. They are about personal data, about boundaries in communication and about how not to get caught by scammers.

Share your work and personal life

Stage name

The nickname must not match your name, surname, nicknames in personal social networks and mail. Do not take the date of birth, the name of the city or the nickname of the pet that friends know. The same applies to the name that you represent in a live broadcast.

Separate accounts

  • Separate email for work only.
  • Working social networks, if needed, start from scratch, without connection with personal accounts and with the phone number that friends know.
  • Reliable unique password and two-factor authentication, if any.

Clean frame

Before the live broadcast, look at the frame through the eyes of a stranger. Is it not visible from the window of a recognizable view, are there any documents, envelopes with an address, forms from work, noticeable things in the frame? Remove everything that can help you find you. How to prepare the space, is described in detail in the article about working from home.

blocking countries and regions

geo-blocking is one of the main privacy tools on STRIPCHAT. In the model account settings, you can specify the countries where your profile and live broadcasts will not be visible. For some countries, individual regions can also be blocked.

Usually models cover their region or the whole country, as well as places where relatives and acquaintances live. Do it immediately after registration, even before the first live broadcast.

Geo-blocking reduces the risk, but does not give a 100% guarantee: a person can end up in another country or bypass the blocking by technical means. Therefore, use it with the rest of the measures.

Never provide personal data

The rule is simple: neither in a live broadcast, nor in private messages, nor in the profile should be anything that connects the model with you in everyday life. These include:

  • real name and surname, city, district, educational institution, place of work;
  • phone number, personal accounts in social networks and messengers;
  • photo where documents, car number, signs near the house are visible;
  • bank card and payment account data.

Even the little things told in different live broadcasts can form into a recognizable picture. Come up with neutral answers in advance to typical questions like “Where are you from” and “What you do”.

Your limits and the right to say no

The work of the model is voluntary, and this applies to every live broadcast, not just the decision to start. What to include in the tip menu and what is acceptable in a private show, you decide. The viewer can offer any amount, the decision is still yours.

  • Write down your boundaries for yourself before the first live broadcast so as not to decide on the move under pressure.
  • Refuse short and calm, without excuses: "No, this will not happen."
  • Block and remove from the chat without hesitation. This is an ordinary working tool, there is no rudeness in it.
  • If something goes wrong, a private show or live broadcast can be completed at any time.

If someone outside the platform pressures you and forces you to work, the situation is serious. Contact the platform’s support team and the police.

How to recognize scammers

Fraudsters hunt both spectators and models. The most common schemes are:

  • “I will pay outside the platform”. After a direct translation, the model usually does not receive anything, but her data is from the scammer.
  • Fake support. He writes supposedly “Administrator” and asks to send a password, code or photo of documents. Real password support does not ask.
  • “Agents” and “studios” promising guaranteed income. If they want access to your account, a share of your payouts or advance payment for “training”, it is better to decline.
  • blackmail, that is, threats to "tell friends" if you don't pay. Do not pay, save evidence and write to support.
  • phishing links. Do not open links from viewers and do not enter account data on third-party sites.

and about money separately: withdraw it only through the model cabinet. What are the ways, described in the article About payments.

If something still went wrong

Even with all precautions, troubles happen. Someone recorded a live broadcast and posted it on a third-party site, a friend recognized you, the viewer began to pursue you in other services. At such a moment, it is important not to panic and act in order.

  1. Preserve all evidence: links, screenshots, usernames and dates. Do not delete the messages, even if they are upsetting.
  2. Do not argue or pay those who require money for silence or removal.
  3. Write to Stripchat support, describe the situation and attach evidence. Leaking live recordings violates the rules of the platform.
  4. If the content is posted on another site, file a complaint through its form of infringement. Many sites remove materials at the request of the copyright holder.
  5. Review the settings: geo-blocking, passwords, linked mail.

Don't blame yourself. The one who did it is responsible for the leak or blackmail.

Where to go for help

STRIPCHAT has a support service for models. Write there about violations of the rules, threats, content leakage and problems with the account. Save screenshots and nicknames of violators, they are analyzed faster. If the threat is real and concerns your life outside of a live broadcast, contact the police. And if you're just starting, go through the steps from the instructions "How to start" and keep these rules in mind.

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to hide live broadcasts from viewers from their country or city?

possible. In the model settings on STRIPCHAT there is a blocking by country, and for some countries and for individual regions. Viewers from blocked places will not see either a live broadcast or a profile.

What to do if the viewer is threatening or blackmailing?

Don't negotiate and pay nothing. Save screenshots, block the user and report to the platform support. In case of real threat, contact the police.

Is the model obligated to fulfill the requests of the audience?

is not required. What to do in a live broadcast, you decide, and you can refuse any request, even if the viewer is ready to pay.

Is it worth switching to messengers at the request of the viewer?

Better not. Outside the platform, its protection does not apply to you, and with such a transition, personal data leaks or fraud often begin.
