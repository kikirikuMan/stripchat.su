Webcam model equipment: where to start and what to improve

First base, then upgrade

Webcam model hardware is not required to be expensive. The audience forgives a simple camera, but from the live broadcast, where the face is in the shade, the sound wheezes and the picture freezes now and then, they leave. Therefore, at the start, it is enough to close the basic needs, you do not need to buy all the best.

It's best to start with what's already there, to do a few live broadcasts and see what's stopping you from it. It is worth improving this weak point. Below, all the elements are disassembled in order, with options for a modest budget and for the next step.

Camera

Built-in laptop camera is only suitable for understanding whether you like the format. Her resolution is usually low, and in dim light she does not cope well.

  • For starters, an external webcam with Full HD resolution (1080p) on a monitor or tripod is enough. This is a standard that is sufficient for most models.
  • Next step: The camera with a better matrix and manual exposure settings and white balance or mirrorless camera through a capture card. The difference is noticeable, but the setting will take time.

Whatever the camera, disable the automatic modes that change the brightness all the time and fix the settings for your light. Put the camera at approximately eye level or a little higher, the angle from below does not suit anyone.

Light

Beginners think about light the least, although the return from it is the largest. With good lighting, the picture becomes clearer, more natural, and the noise disappears even at an inexpensive camera.

  • A ring lamp or LED panel in front of the face is suitable for a start, and in the daytime there is also light from the window. The window should be on the side or in front, but not behind the back.
  • Next step: two sources with softboxes on either side of the camera and weak background illumination. Sharp shadows disappear, and the image looks more voluminous.

Take the lamps that adjust the brightness and color temperature to adjust to the day and evening. It is better not to mix warm and cold light in one frame, otherwise the camera will begin to get confused with the white balance.

Microphone and sound

Many viewers are also listening to the live broadcast. Built-in laptop microphone catches fan rumble, room echo and keyboard knock.

  • A looping microphone or a simple USB microphone on a stand is enough to get started. Speech immediately becomes much more legible.
  • Next step: A good USB mic with a directional diagram that hears the room less. Echo will help remove the carpet and thick curtains.

Before the live broadcast, record a few seconds of speech and listen in headphones. So the easiest way is to hear yourself the way the audience hears.

Internet and computer

The most important thing for broadcasting is upload (upload), and for normal surfing, they look at the download speed. For many tariffs, the load is fast, and the return is slow, and the picture crumbles into squares just because of it.

  • Check the recoil on any speed measurement service during those hours when you are going to live streaming.
  • Connect the computer to the router with a cable. Wi-Fi is convenient, but the speed jumps on it and there are breaks.
  • For the duration of the live broadcast, close the cloud sync, torrents and updates.
  • Platform recommendations on the quality of broadcast are in the live broadcast settings and in the reference for models.

Any modern computer will do without jerking video communication. If you are using the overlay program, watch the CPU load: when it is overloaded, the video starts to twitch.

Space arrangement

The room in the frame tells about you no less than the appearance. The designer interior is useless, a fairly neat, calm background.

  • Remove everything personal from the frame: photos of relatives, documents, envelopes with an address, a view from the window by which you can find out the area.
  • Plain wall, fabric or screen cheaply solve the issue with the background.
  • Put a comfortable seat and keep the water on hand, live streaming is long.
  • Warn your family about live broadcast time and close the door so no one gets into the frame by accident.

Checklist before live broadcast

  1. The light is on, the face is illuminated evenly, there is no shadow.
  2. The camera is at eye level, manual settings are fixed.
  3. The microphone is checked, there is no extraneous noise.
  4. The computer is connected by cable, the extra programs are closed.
  5. There are no personal items and details in the frame by which you can understand where you are.

Frequently asked questions

Is it possible to start working as a model from a phone?

You can try if the phone has a good camera and a tripod. For constant work, a computer with a separate webcam is more convenient, it is easier to follow the chat and settings with it.

What is more important to buy in the first place: a camera or a light?

Most often light. An inexpensive camera in good light gives a decent picture, and an expensive one in a dark room still makes noise.

Why does the broadcast slow down when the Internet is fast?

For live broadcast, the speed of return (not loading) and its stability are important. Check the recoil, connect the computer with cable instead of Wi-Fi and close the programs that load the network.
