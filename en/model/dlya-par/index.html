Webcam work for couples: agree before the first stream

Webcams for couples: What's special about

Webcams for couples is a separate format. There are two live broadcasts, and viewers come to see how you communicate with each other and what you do together. It is interesting to work as a webcam couple, but the fact that a single-player model decides with herself, the couple has to discuss together: the rules, money, privacy, and even what will happen if you break one day.

General registration steps are described in the guide how to start. Here we will talk about what concerns couples.

Both partners: 18+ and document verification

There are no exceptions to this rule: everyone who appears on camera must be an adult and verified on the platform. This applies to both partners, not just the account holder. An unverified partner cannot briefly appear in the broadcast, wave to viewers or pass through the background.

Prepare the valid documents of both in advance and pass the test before the first live broadcast. If other people live with you, make sure that they do not fall into the frame and into the microphone.

Consent: mutual and revocable

A couple of webcam models can only work if both want it. Phrases like "I agreed because he asked" or "let's try for money, and then we'll see" means that there really is no agreement. If one persuades the other, presses or offends the refusal, you should not start.

Consent is not given forever. Each of you can say "stop" at any moment: in the middle of a live broadcast, a month or a year later. Agree on this in advance, then such a conversation will not turn into a scandal.

borders worth negotiating

It is better to discuss them on the shore until the chat started asking for something new right during the show. It is convenient to make a general list:

  • What are you doing in a live broadcast

    formats that suit both and what is not discussed for any tip. Everyone has the right to refuse.

  • Stop signal

    Word or gesture, after which live broadcast or action is terminated without question.

  • Solo live broadcasts

    Can any of you go live separately, and if so, from which account.

  • Communication with viewers

    Who responds in the chat and how to respond to jealousy, flirting and requests from the audience to one of you.

Return to the list every few weeks. Over time, the sensations change, there is nothing strange in this.

Privacy Together

The couple has twice as many acquaintances, relatives and colleagues who can accidentally stumble upon a live broadcast. Therefore, privacy should be taken care of here especially carefully:

  • Select a common stage name that is not related to your names.
  • Set the blocking of countries and regions where your family and friends live.
  • Remove from the frame the things you can find an apartment or city.
  • Agree that no one publishes joint materials without the consent of the second.

How to protect personal data is described in detail in on safety.

Distribute responsibilities

The time in the frame of the couple is only part of the work. Someone needs to adjust the camera and light, update the profile and tip menu, respond to regular viewers between live broadcasts and monitor the schedule. If all this is silently gets alone, in a couple of months there will be an insult, even if the money is divided equally.

Speak who is responsible for what and consider this inconspicuous work when you decide how to share income. Well, if both are able to start the broadcast and know where the necessary settings are in the office. Then the live broadcast will not fail if one is ill or delayed.

How to honestly share income

Payouts come to the details of the account for. It turns out that the second partner legally depends on the first, and the agreement on money should be clear to both.

  1. Decide how to share income (equally, by participation or otherwise) and how you take into account the cost of equipment.
  2. Write down the agreement. A simple note signed by both is more reliable than "we trust each other."
  3. Make the accruals transparent so that both see how much came and how much has been withdrawn.
  4. Agree who and how pay taxes on income. How the withdrawal of money is arranged, disassembled in the guide on payments.

if the relationship ends

It is unpleasant to think about it at the beginning, but at the beginning such a conversation is most calm. Discuss in advance:

  • Who accounts for the account, pseudonym and regular viewers.
  • How are the last accruals and money that have not yet been withdrawn.
  • What to do with recordings and photos, where there is a second partner. They should be removed from the profile at his first request.
  • How to tell viewers about changes and whether it is necessary at all.

Do not use shared material against a former partner, threaten to publish it or publish it without consent. In many countries this can also carry criminal liability.

It is more difficult for two to match in time than alone. Choose permanent days and hours that are suitable for both, including work, study and fatigue. Leave time when you're just a couple and don't discuss live broadcasts. Collaboration easily begins to eat up relationships if one is not separated from the other.

The income of a couple, like any model, is not guaranteed and depends on the regularity, communication and time in the live broadcast. If you discussed everything, both are confident in the decision and passed the document check, you can register.

Frequently asked questions

Do both partners need to be verified?

Yes. Everyone who appears in a broadcast must be an adult and pass document verification. An unverified partner must not appear on camera, even briefly.

Is it possible to work as a pair if one of the partners doubts?

It's better not to start. Joint live broadcasts are possible only with the voluntary and conscious consent of both. Discuss doubts in advance and remember that consent can be revoked at any time.

To whom to issue payments in pairs?

Payments come to the details of the account owner who has passed the verification. Therefore, agree in advance on how to divide the income, and write down the agreement.

What will happen to the couple's account after parting?

The account is left to who it is registered to. In order not to quarrel later, decide the fate of your account and live broadcast records even before the start, while you are doing well.
